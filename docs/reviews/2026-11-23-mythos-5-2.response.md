## The headline, said plainly

**The review is right, and its finding is about silence.** Every hostile input it tried was refused by name. What passed were checks that never ran:
- a gate behind an optional key (A2, and `fab_tolerances` in A15);
- a decoded register field nobody compares (A12);
- a screen fed a declared number in place of the drawn copper (A4);
- a convention derived for one connector family and applied to another (A1).

**Sentinel-03, as committed, is not ready to fabricate, and nothing in the toolchain would have said so.** A1–A7 each passed `validate`, `solve`, `emit` and `extract`. Four of them would have been found with the board in hand.

**A8 is the one confident wrong answer:** `replay` printed `pass`, `matched: true` for an archive whose own records show a register mismatch. `RUL-19` (3) says replay proves integrity, and that archive is not internally consistent.

**A9 and A10 matter more than their size.** The bench is the project's only contact with reality, and nothing yet pins that the board on the bench is the board in the repository.

**On order:**
- the backlog wave running now (`4300j`, `1323`, `4408`, `1916`) finishes;
- **`AET-WO-2312`** (replay and attribute bound to the records) and **`AET-WO-3308`** (freeze what was ordered) are written with this response and launch now: their fences miss the running wave's, except `ci/route.sh` beside `1916`;
- **`AET-WO-4410`** (hwdef and pincheck, roadmap §5 wave 2) and **`AET-WO-4300k`** (Sentinel-03 fabrication readiness) follow `4300j`, as planned, not yet written;
- **`AET-WO-1609`** (the ran-or-not table) follows `1608`, and **`AET-WO-1917`** (CI) is planned;
- **no Sentinel-03 package is made until `4300k`'s gate passes on the committed specification.** That gate's hwdef clause needs `4410`, so `4300k`'s package step waits for both.

## For Scotty

- **A5, the pack ceiling.** Either declare Sentinel-03 at most 4S, or move the input clamp (the TVS row, L935, owned by `4500`) ahead of its package. Twice a full 4S pack is 33.6 V, under VIN's 38 V. The TVS row already says one fits 3S–4S and none fits 6S. **Lean: 4S until the clamp is designed.** *Ruled by Scotty, 2026-11-23: at most 4S now, a 6S variant intended later.*
- **BOR level 3 against the flash** (a question). Level 3's falling threshold (2.54–2.68 V, the review's figure) lies below the SPI flash's 2.7 V floor, and there is no higher level. The choices are a supervisor, or the window recorded as a named condition. Round 2 ruled level 3 right for a flight board (A3), and that ruling stands either way.
- **B4, a crystal on Sentinel-03.** The HSE path would meet our own copper before Sentinel-02 depends on it, at the cost of one part, two oscillator nets and area on a cost-constrained board. Your call; `4300k` carries whichever you rule. *Ruled by Scotty, 2026-11-23: yes, the crystal goes on Sentinel-03; the architect agrees.*

- **Amending `RUL-14` without sweeping its optional keys.** `drop_limits` and `fab_tolerances` stayed optional after 2026-11-17, and their consequence for Sentinel-03 was never recorded.
- **Leaving A3 in three FEEDBACK files with no roadmap row.** This is the same error round 2 named.
- **Writing `examples/sentinel-03*.json` into the brief.** The next board to be fabricated was never promoted out of a test fixture.
- **Treating the order as settled when nothing pinned it,** and leaving a bench sheet that names a hash on the docs-only route.
