# Aethelgard — third independent review (Mythos 5.2, round 3)

**Reviewed:** `origin/main` at `7fbe5d11d4474ff37b56ac3662fee342a50dd783`, 2026-11-23, read-only, in `~/aethelgard-review3`.
**Reviewer:** Claude Mythos 5.2, fresh context, with six sub-reviewers (one indexer, five on code). Every sub-reviewer claim I repeat is labelled "sub", and says whether I re-ran it.

## Executive summary

1. **Sentinel-03, as committed, is not ready to fabricate.** Seven reasons, six checked on the host and one resting on a sub-reviewer's model (A5): the XT30 battery connector is placed with its mating face pointing into the board (A1); the supply gate does not run on it (A2); `generate hwdef` refuses it, so ArduPilot has never been judged against it (A3); three electrical items in its power section are printed but not blocked (A4–A6); and its stackup and tolerances cite two different fabricators (A7).
2. **The engine is hard to fool from the front.** 25 mutated Sentinel-03 specifications, a board with a bus's copper removed, hostile placer inputs, archives and scenarios were all refused by name. 36 datasheet citations in the power tables matched held text.
3. **The gaps are at the seams:** a check that is optional, a screen fed a declared number, a convention derived for one part family and applied to another.
4. **One confident wrong answer in `replay`:** `pass`, `matched: true` for an archive whose own records show a register mismatch (A8).
5. **Sentinel-01's ordered boards:** the Gerbers have not moved since the order, but nothing pins that, and the bench sheet names an ELF two regenerations old (A9, A10).
6. **Every generated ArduPilot board turns SWD off at boot** (A11).
7. **CI and the sandbox held.** One low gap in docs-only routing (A16).
8. **Earlier reviews' fixes that I checked are real.** The backlog table has stale rows (A14).
9. **Top three (Part C):** a fabrication-readiness gate before Sentinel-03's package; make a check that did not run as loud as one that failed; freeze what was ordered.

What was not read is listed at the end: it includes the twin, `svd-hal`'s generator, the router and `verify.rs`. No container was run.

### A1. The battery connector faces into the board — high — new
- **Evidence (sub; re-checked on my own `emit`).** `aethelgard emit --file crates/aethelgard-cli/tests/fixtures/sentinel-03.json` places J4 (`AMASS_XT30PW-M_1x02_P2.50mm_Horizontal`) at (58.05, 78.15), rotation 270. The outline's left edge is x = 55.75.
  - The through-hole tails are 2.3 mm from the edge.
  - The footprint's F.Fab body spans local y −13.1 to −4.1. At rotation 270 that is x 62.15 to 71.15, inside the board. The mating face is the end away from the tails, so it points inward.
- **Cause.** `place_connectors` (`crates/aethelgard-eda/src/pcb.rs:1453-1463`, 1515, 1525) puts every connector's courtyard +y side on the edge. `connector_edges` (`pcb.rs:758-760`) calls that "cable side out". The convention was derived for the JST GH parts, whose tails are at −y. The XT30's tails are at +y.
- **Why no gate sees it.** The tests (`pcb.rs:3066-3097`, 3179-3244, 3265-3292) assert only that a courtyard edge is on the board edge, and only for the four JST parts. ERC, DRC and `extract` have no notion of a mating face.
- **Confidence:** medium-high. The geometry is measured. "The opening is opposite the tails" comes from the footprint and from memory of the XT30PW drawing. The drawing is held at `~/.cache/aet-ds/aet-2304/amass-xt30pw-m/xt30pw-m-spec.pdf`, but this host has no PDF renderer, so I could not read it.
- **Recommendation.** Make the mating side a per-footprint fact in the connector tables, assert it for every connector and power-input part, and check the AMASS drawing before any order.

### A2. Sentinel-03's supply gate does not run — high — partly known
- **Reproduction (mine).** `extract` on the committed specification's board prints `judged: 8` and no `supplies` key. With `power.drop_limits` (165 mV on `VDD_3V3`) and the four stackup minima added to a copy (`target/r3/probe/s03-gated.json`, the same fields `supply_tier1.rs:49-60` adds), `extract` takes five minutes and prints `judged: 9` with a `supplies` list of one entry.
- **Evidence (sub; re-checked).** `crates/aethelgard-eda/src/supply.rs:1349-1356` returns no gates when `power.drop_limits` is absent. `aethelgard-ir` validates the key's shape (`power.rs:863-878`) and never requires it. The committed fixture's `power` keys are `conditions, input, battery, regulators, loads, sense, crossings`. `grep -rln drop_limits crates/*/tests examples scenarios` finds only `supply_tier1.rs` and `ir/tests/power.rs`; `supply_tier1.rs:58` and `:247` add the key to a copy.
- **The claim it contradicts.** `docs/roadmap.md:1003`: "`IMU_SCK` 24.9 pF, the gate passes (85.8 mV)". That is true of the test's copy, not of the specification a package would be made from.
- **Why it is invisible.** My `aethelgard extract` run on the emitted board printed `verdict: pass, judged: 8`, with no supply entry and no "nothing in scope" marker, because the capacitance lines were judged.
- **Rule broken.** `RUL-14`, amended 2026-11-17: "A key whose absence would relax a check stays required." The optional key is a ruled choice (`1303b` §1.4; `1209` FEEDBACK:99). Its consequence for Sentinel-03 is not recorded.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Recommendation.** Require `drop_limits` wherever a power tree is declared, or have `verify` refuse a package for such a board without it.

### A3. ArduPilot cannot be judged against Sentinel-03 today — high — known in FEEDBACK, unowned
- **Evidence (run).** `aethelgard solve --file …/sentinel-03.json > topo.json; aethelgard generate hwdef --topology topo.json --out x` prints `{"ok":false,"error":{"kind":"SenseNotEmitted","id":"VBAT_SENSE",…}}`.
- **Consequence.** No `hwdef.dat` from `main`, so no ArduPilot build, no `check pinout` and no `simulate --foreign` for the board that `roadmap.md:375` says "proves the sensors Sentinel-02 will fly".
- **Status.** Recorded in `4400b` D7, `4201` F2 and `4202` FEEDBACK:197. `grep SenseNotEmitted docs/roadmap.md` finds nothing: no §7.1 row and no owner. `AET-WO-4408`, in flight, does not cover it.
- **Recommendation.** A work order for the battery-monitor lines before Sentinel-03's package. The divider is already in `power.sense`.

### A4. The buck's thermal screen rests on copper the board does not have — high — new (sub; zones re-checked)
- **Evidence.** `solve` prints: "U10: its junction is screened, not guaranteed: 1170 mW … at θJA 28.0 °C/W, the figure's point at 20 cm² for the 20 cm² per layer declared". The fixture's own source for the area is "declared by the fixture … not measured on the drawn board".
  - The emitted board has two zones only: `GND` on `In1.Cu` and `VDD_3V3` on `In2.Cu`. Neither outer layer has a pour.
  - `crates/aethelgard-ir/src/power.rs:2709` compares the layer count only.
- **Size.** The stated margin is 7.24 K at an 85 °C board. From the sub-reviewer, unverified by me: TI's JEDEC θJA for the package is about 43 °C/W, which would put the junction near 135 °C.
- **Confidence:** high on the board facts, medium on the consequence.
- **Recommendation.** Measure the copper joined to the thermal pad from the drawn board, as `extract` already reads copper, or draw the pours. Until then the 85 °C ceiling is unsupported.

### A5. Hot-plugging a 6S pack is not shown to stay under 38 V — high — known gap, new numbers (sub)
- **Evidence.** `power.rs:2535-2546` checks the bulk capacitor's voltage rating and a 20–100 µF value. Its ESR is printed and compared with nothing. `solve`'s own condition: "the ring's size is not computed, and VIN's absolute maximum is 38000 mV". A 6S pack is 26.1 V.
- **The sub-reviewer's model** (a model, not a measurement; 300 nH of lead; `target/r3/power/ring.py`) puts the pin between 31 and 46 V depending on ESR. The part row holds ESR at 20 °C and −40 °C only, and the board's declared floor is −20 °C.
- **Status.** `roadmap.md:933` (a TVS row owned by `4500`) and `:1081`.
- **Recommendation.** Declare Sentinel-03 as at most 4S, where twice the pack is under 38 V, or move the clamp ahead of its package.

### A6. Input capacitance is 4.09 µF effective against TI's 10 µF — medium — known
- **Evidence.** `solve`: "U10: `cin` holds 4.092 µF effective at 26100 mV under DC bias". It is a condition, not a refusal (`roadmap.md:931`; `4300d3` D2 (c)).
- **Recommendation.** Fix it on Sentinel-03 itself, with more or larger input ceramics, instead of carrying it to `4500`.

### A7. Sentinel-03's stackup and tolerances cite two fabricators — low — new
- **Evidence (read).** `board.stackup.dielectrics` cite OSH Park's 4-layer service (0.179 mm prepreg minimum). `board.stackup.fab_tolerances` cite JLCPCB. `roadmap.md:375` names "a JLC quote" as a cost lever. Nothing binds the two blocks to one maker, and `validate` accepts the mix.
- **Why it matters.** `extract`'s capacitance figure is an upper bound only if each dielectric is at least its declared minimum. A thinner prepreg (JLCPCB offers one near 0.1 mm, from memory) would void the bound.
- **What already limits it.** Sentinel-01's `FAB-NOTES.md` (lines 34–42) states the stackup and says "A board made outside this stackup is not covered by the capacitance bound". So the minima do travel with the package, and the risk is an orderer picking a maker's default stackup without reading them.
- **Recommendation.** One declared fabricator that every bound cites, or a `validate` refusal when the blocks' sources differ.

### A8. `replay` passes an archive whose records show a failure — medium — mostly new (sub; re-run)
- **Evidence (re-run).** `aethelgard replay --archive target/r3/chaos/out/reg_unnamed/10019a7ca6fb9f96d75fdeaef6f47765` prints `"verdict":"pass","expect":"pass","matched":true,"findings":[]`, exit 0. The archive's records hold `GPIOB.AFRL` observed `0x47000000` against an expected `0x44000000`, with the verdict record left empty. `attribute` on the same archive says `indeterminate`, rule `I1`.
- **Scope.** `check_run` (`crates/aethelgard-chaos/src/campaign.rs:1008-1465`) binds no records for `register_mismatch`, `i2c_timeout`, `spi_timeout`, the identity, configure and data mismatches, `*_record_invalid`, `cpu_halted` or `locked_peripheral_access`. Three more shapes behave the same; their archives are beside the first.
- **Status.** `roadmap.md:1109` and `AET-WO-2310.md` item 7 cover only the `*_transaction_unwitnessed` case. `RUL-19` (3) says replay proves integrity. This archive is internally inconsistent and still replays as a pass.
- **Recommendation.** Derive these kinds' counts from the records in one function shared by `replay` and `attribute`, so the two cannot disagree.

### A9. Nothing pins what was sent to the fabricator — medium — new
- **Evidence (run).** `git diff --stat ee835bde HEAD -- fab/sentinel-01-bench/package` shows only `FAB-NOTES.md`, `MANIFEST.json`, the JLCPCB BOM and the ELF changed. `ee835bde` is the last package commit on the order date, 2026-11-19. No Gerber or drill byte moved, and all 16 files match `MANIFEST.json` today.
- **The gap.** That result is not held by any check. CI's `--against-fab` step re-derives the package, so a work order that moves copper recommits it and stays green. No file holds the hashes that were ordered. I assumed the order used the tree as of that commit; nothing in the repository says which commit it was.
- **Recommendation.** A committed list of the ordered copper, mask, paste, edge and drill hashes, with a tier-1 test that fails when the package differs. A deliberate re-spin then edits that list by ruling.

### A10. The bench sheet names an ELF that is no longer in the package — low — new
- **Evidence (run).** `fab/sentinel-01-bench/BENCH.md:5` gives the ELF's sha256 as `008a2a59…`. The committed ELF is `f3e871bb…`, regenerated at `943f7112` and again at `8fdc9422`. No test reads `BENCH.md`, and `ci/route.sh` classes it docs-only.
- **Risk.** The sheet's predicted RAM words were written against an earlier image. Two rows were updated on 2026-11-20; nothing shows the rest still hold.
- **Recommendation.** A test comparing the sheet's hash with `MANIFEST.json`. That also takes `BENCH.md` off the docs-only route.

### A11. Every generated hwdef turns SWD off — medium — new consequence (sub; grep re-run)
- **Evidence.** `grep -c 'PA13\|PA14\|SWD'` is 0 for `crates/aethelgard-hwdef/src/lib.rs` and for every committed `hwdef.dat` fixture. ArduPilot makes an unlisted pin an input, and `AET-WO-4400a.FEEDBACK.md:32` records PA13 and PA14 as inputs in the built ELF.
- **Consequence (inferred, not measured).** Once ArduPilot boots, a debugger attaches only under reset. The boards have no bootloader and no USB port in the serial order.
- **Recommendation.** Emit `PA13 JTMS-SWDIO SWD` and `PA14 JTCK-SWCLK SWD`.

### A12. `check pinout` does not judge a chip select's reset level — medium — known
- **Evidence (sub).** `crates/aethelgard-pincheck/src/lib.rs:514` tests the mode only. `odr` is decoded at `elf.rs:307` and never used. A kit ELF with GPIOA `ODR` changed from `0xffff` to `0xfffc`, both selects low, passes with no finding. The crate's README lists `ODR` under what it cannot see.
- **Recommendation.** Compare `ODR` on the topology's GPIO pins. The expected bit already exists.

### A13. Power rules that accept wrong designs — medium — mixed (sub; two re-run)
Each is a mutation of Sentinel-03 that `validate` and `solve` accept (`python3 -I target/r3/power/mut.py <name>`):
- **A sense divider of 10 MΩ over 1 MΩ** (`sense_huge`, re-run, rc 0), or 100 Ω over 10 Ω, which is about 6 W in an 0603 (`power.rs:2177-2229`). New.
- **No loads at all** (`no_loads_at_all`, re-run, rc 0): the 5 V rail carries 0 mA. Partly known (`1209` A3, A4).
- **A declared loss not tied to its load.** U11's 550 mW is sourced at 679 mA while the solved load is 747 mA, and a declared 1 mW is accepted. Known by design (`4300d3` F9).
- **Peak inductor current** is never compared with the switch limit's minimum (`power.rs:1457-1471`). New. Sentinel-03 itself is inside it.
- **A false line in Sentinel-03's own verdict:** "the supply currents of U3, U7, U4, U5 are not in its load", although `loads` names all four (`power.rs:3058-3075`). New, low.
- **Recorded nowhere:** the board has no reverse-polarity protection or fuse, and BOR level 3's falling threshold (2.54–2.68 V) lies below the flash's 2.7 V floor.

### A15. Smaller items (sub, not re-run)

- **The foreign judge** can pass a run in which ArduPilot panics after its last witness (`crates/aethelgard-cli/src/renode/foreign.rs:1265-1300`). Partly known (`foreign.rs:51`). Unverified: no Renode on the host.

## Part C: three recommendations, ranked

1. **Hold Sentinel-03's package until a readiness gate passes on the committed specification itself (B1; A1–A7).** All seven findings passed `validate`, `solve`, `emit` and `extract` today. Four would be found with the board in hand: a battery plug that faces the wrong way, a regulator hotter than screened, an input that may ring past its absolute maximum, and a flight firmware never judged against the board. The gate is mostly refusals over data the engine already holds.

2. **Make a check that did not run as loud as one that failed (B5; A2, A12, A15).** The project's strongest habit is refusing by name. The false passes that remain are silences: a gate behind an optional key, a decoded register field nobody compares, a screen fed a declared number. One table in every verdict, with `RUL-14`'s own rule applied to `drop_limits` and `fab_tolerances`, closes the class and not just these instances.

3. **Freeze what was ordered, and tie the bench sheet to it (A9, A10).** The bench comparison is the project's only contact with reality. It is worth as much as the certainty that the board on the bench is the board in the repository. One hash list and two small tests buy that.
