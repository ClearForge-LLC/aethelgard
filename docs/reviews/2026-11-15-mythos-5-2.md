## Executive summary

1. The discipline the first review praised still holds: cited parts data, fail-closed exits, the strict reader, extraction's allowlist, the uid boundary. A subagent checked every transcribed datasheet value it could against held text and found **no wrong number**.
2. The gap has moved. The gates are now honest about the board's copper. They are not yet honest about the **silicon and the support circuitry**: four facts would reach a fabricated board with every gate green.
3. **A1 (reproduced):** the generated firmware's SPI1 has no kernel clock on real silicon (RM0433: `SPI123SEL` resets to `pll1_q_ck`, PLL1 off). The twin still "witnesses" the flash read. Baud rate and slave-select are also left at reset.
4. **A2 (reproduced):** the emitted board gives the STM32H753 one 100 nF capacitor for the 16 pins it has on the rail, no reset capacitor, no programming connector and no supply source. `verify --fab-out` writes its package anyway.
5. **A3:** the declared brown-out level decides the droop verdict, but nothing programs the option byte; the factory default is level 0.
6. **A4:** the power tree passes with no check of battery hot-plug overshoot, buck self-heating or capacitor DC-bias (named or ruled, not judged).
7. **A5 is the root cause of A1, A8 and A10:** the Kani oracle is generated from the same calls as the code it proves, and Renode ignores the same fields. `INV-07` holds in letter, not independently.
8. **A6:** on a motor board `verify` passes, and can write a fabrication package, with the whole-schedule proof skipped. Sentinel-02 is a motor board.
9. **A7:** `main`'s engine-image workflow has failed on all 12 pushes since `4100` (2026-11-14). The first review's "quickstart on `main`" fix is red and unread.
10. First-review ledger: most adopted fixes are real (A1, A2, A3 and A5 re-attacked and held); A6 regressed; A8.9 was never fixed; A4 holds only through `verify`.
11. Tests are strong: 7 of 8 mutants on new guards died in their own crate, and the eighth is caught one crate up (read, not run). A 5,045-mutant numeric fuzz found no crash.
12. One new wrong pass in a gate: `check drc --spec` judges a coupled pair without seeing arcs (reproduced; `verify` is protected by `extract`).
13. Top three: a day of hardware truth before more surface (fix A1–A4, run the blank 3302 bench sheet); a witness ledger so unwitnessed silicon facts are visible; judge the pair that flies (ArduPilot in the twin, the motor-board proof) before `4500`.
14. Not covered: the placer in `pcb.rs`, the chaos and evolve changes; `attribute`, `replay` and `campaign` were not re-attacked; the 2205 B1–B3 replay lead was not reproduced.

## A1. On real silicon the generated firmware's SPI1 has no kernel clock; the twin witnesses a flash read the board cannot perform — high, new

- **Evidence (run).** `generate` on the reference, `build`, then `simulate --platform sentinel-01.repl --firmware fw.elf --spec … --for 100ms`: `verdict: pass`. `boot.spi_witness[0]` for `U4` lists `spi1.spi1mux.u4: Transmitting data 0x9F …`, expected and observed.
- **Evidence (read).** In the generated crate the only `RCC` accesses are four enables (`board/src/board.rs:575-593`: `AHB4ENR` bits 0 and 1, `APB1LENR` bit 21, `APB2ENR` bit 12). The solved `clock` is "the reset clock, HSI at 64 MHz" (`crates/aethelgard-ir/README.md:136`). The solver puts the flash on `SPI1`.
- **Reference manual (read from the held PDF, RM0433 Rev 9).** "`SPI123SEL[2:0]` … 000: pll1_q_ck clock selected as SPI/I2S1,2 and 3 kernel clock (default after reset)". "`PLL1ON` … 0: PLL1 OFF (default after reset)". So SPI1, SPI2 and SPI3 produce no SCK until firmware starts PLL1 or selects `per_ck`.
- **Same family (confirmed at `crates/aethelgard-svd-hal/src/board.rs:2014-2027`).** The generated doc comment says: "The baud rate, clock polarity and phase, and slave-select management are not solved yet: they stay at reset (Renode 1.17.0 does not model them)".
  - `MBR` stays 000, so SCK is kernel/2 whatever `speed_hz` declares and `check speeds` judges.
  - `SSM` and `SSI` stay 0 in master mode. A low NSS input then raises a mode fault (that consequence is from memory).
- **What it is not.** ArduPilot sets its own clocks. This fails the bring-up firmware at first power-on, loudly, not the flight stack. It matters because the verdict says the opposite of what the bench will see.
- **Not recorded.** `rg SPI123SEL` finds nothing in `docs/` or the code. Roadmap §7.1 has no row for SPI clock, baud or NSS. The 3302 bench sheet has no SPI row.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Recommendation.**
  - Select `per_ck` for SPI123, or solve the kernel clock as `4300a` did for timers (extend the run-time `ResetClock` check to `D2CCIP1R`).
  - Write `MBR` from `speed_hz` and judge the achieved rate. Set `SSM|SSI`.
  - Add an SPI row (SCK on PA5) to the 3302 sheet.

## A2. The generated board gives the MCU one 100 nF capacitor for all its supply pins; no gate judges support circuitry — high, new

- **Evidence (run).** The reference BOM: `C1,C2 2u2` (the two VCAPs), `C3..C6 100n` (one per IC), `R1,R2 2k2`, `R3..R5 10k`. The net list read from the emitted `.kicad_pcb` (`target/exp/nets.py`) shows:
  - `VDD_3V3` carries 16 `U1` pins (6, 17, 30, 32, 33, 39, 52, 62, 72, 84, 95, 108, 121, 131, 143, 144) and four 100 nF capacitors for the whole board;
  - `NRST` (U1.25) is unconnected;
  - `PA13` and `PA14` (U1.105, U1.109) are unconnected and there is no connector, so nothing can program the part (`BOOT0` is correctly grounded);
  - `VDD_3V3` has no source.
- **Pin names (read).** Positions 25, 105, 109, 138 and 143 are `NRST`, `PA13 (JTMS/SWDIO)`, `PA14 (JTCK/SWCLK)`, `BOOT0` and `PDR_ON` in the vendored `STM32H753ZITx.xml`.
- **Evidence (read).** `crates/aethelgard-eda/src/schematic.rs:644` `b.decouple(&mcu_ref, &spec.mcu.supply.clone(), "100n")?;`, once. `AET-WO-2301.FEEDBACK.md:322`: "one 100 nF per IC on the supply". The MCU's row in `power-parts.json` has no `external` list, though the regulator and translator rows do.
- **Datasheet (read, DS12117 Rev 10 §6.1.6, p. 102, Figure 14 notes).** "N corresponds to the number of VDD pins available on the package." "Caution: Each power supply pair (VDD/VSS, VDDA/VSSA ...) must be decoupled with filtering ceramic capacitors as shown above." The figure's values (N × 100 nF plus 4.7 µF, and so on) are in the graphic; from memory, unverified.
- **Why it matters.** ERC and DRC pass, the proofs and the twin never see a capacitor, and the droop gate computes with "the decoupling the BOM declares". `verify --fab-out` writes this board's package (P3 gate clause 3). Sentinel-02 inherits the rule. No work order or backlog row owns MCU decoupling. A debug connector exists as an option for Sentinel-02 (`4300f`); nothing requires one.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Recommendation.** Add `external` roles to the MCU's row (per-pin 100 nF, bulk, VDDA and VREF+, NRST) and emit from it. Add the support gate of B1. Until then FAB-NOTES should say support circuitry is not judged.

## A3. The declared brown-out level decides two gates, but nothing programs it — high, recorded as "out of scope" with no owner

- **Evidence (read).**
  - `crates/aethelgard-ir/src/lib.rs:156-166`: `mcu.bor_level` … "the option-byte write is out of scope". The reference declares `"bor_level": 3`.
  - `rg -i 'option byte|BOR_LEV|OPTSR|bor_level'` over `svd-hal`, `twin`, `eda`, `cli/src/fab.rs` and `cli/src/verify.rs` finds nothing.
  - The project's own bench sheet (`AET-WO-3302.bench.md:131-134`) records RM0433 Table 21's factory default as `BOR_LEV` `00` and predicts the Nucleo reads `0x0`.
- **Consequence.** `droop-bor3` passes with one reset at VBOR3 (2.61 V typical), "recovering within 100 µs". A board flashed with the generated firmware, or with ArduPilot, sits at level 0. It does not reset at 2.61 V and runs on toward 1.62 V, below the flash chip's 2.7 V minimum. `4300d`'s supply-floor margin is likewise against a threshold the part does not have. A declared fact reaches two gates and no artifact (`INV-01`).
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Recommendation.** One of:
  - the firmware reads `FLASH_OPTSR_CUR[3:2]` into its boot record and the judge compares it;
  - the fabrication package carries a factory-flash step that programs and reads back `BOR_LEV`;
  - today, `validate` refuses `bor_level != 0` as "not programmed by anything aethelgard emits".

## A4. The power tree passes with no judgement of hot-plug overshoot, buck self-heating or capacitor DC-bias — high for Sentinel-02; named or ruled, and I disagree

`sentinel-02-power.json` is a test fixture, not Sentinel-02's specification. The checks are the ones that specification will face.
- **Hot-plug (read).** `AET-WO-4300d.FEEDBACK.md:237`: "Named condition: hot-plugging a pack through long leads rings the input above its DC value; TI (p23, p32) asks for 20–100 µF bulk capacitance in parallel then, and the 38 V absolute maximum is the ceiling".
  - `rg -i 'hot-plug|inrush|reverse|TVS'` over `ir/src/power.rs` finds nothing. The condition is in neither a check nor the verdict's conditions.
  - The fixture gives each buck 10 µF plus 0.22 µF of ceramic and no bulk or clamp.
  - A drone pack is hot-plugged on every flight, and 6S HV is 26.1 V. How far it rings is general knowledge, not measured here.
- **Buck temperature (confirmed at `power.rs:1386-1394`).** The only check compares the board's ambient range with the part row's range. For the LMR33630 that row is its junction range, so 85 °C ≤ 125 °C passes with no dissipation term. The LDO has a junction check. For the buck, `AET-WO-4300d.FEEDBACK.md:319` (D1) says RθJA "cannot be used for design" per TI, "so for the buck it would be a named condition"; no such condition is checked. A subagent estimates 118–173 °C for the 5 V buck at its declared 2 A from 6S (its efficiency assumption; unverified).
- **DC bias (`power.rs:1639-1643`).** "judged at rated capacitance … not judged (ruled D6)", stated as a note.
- **Smaller (subagent; the first confirmed at `power.rs:851`).**
  - The BOR floor uses the falling maximum only. A rail must also clear the rising maximum (2.78 V at level 3).
  - The fixture's 3.3 V divider (105 k / 46.4 k) centres at 3.263 V, about 10 mV inside the engine's own band.
  - EN, PG and the translator's OE are listed pins that nothing wires or sequences.
  - HSI tolerance (DS12117 Table 36) is not carried into DShot bit timing.
- **Confidence:** high on the code; medium on the magnitudes.
- **Recommendation.** Make hot-plug a check: a declared bulk or clamp part on a battery input, or a refusal. Give the buck a junction check with a cited or declared loss bound. Use a published DC-bias figure or a stated derating. Add the rising threshold.

## A5. The proof's oracle is generated from the same calls as the code it proves, and the twin ignores the same fields — high (root cause), new as a stated consequence

- **Evidence (confirmed at source).**
  - `crates/aethelgard-svd-hal/src/board.rs:1792` and `:2536` both take `(mode, otype, ospeed, pupd)` from `p.policy()`: once to emit the code, once to emit the oracle.
  - `:3016-3022` writes the oracle's `TIMINGR` from the same `I2C_MODES` table.
  - `:2745-2749` asserts "CFG1 changed beyond DSIZE", which pins the missing baud rate as correct.
- **What it means.** For GPIO mode, output type, speed and pull, for `TIMINGR`, and for SPI configuration, the proof shows the generated code agrees with the generator. Architecture §2.21 and §2.22 list the same fields as ones Renode tags or does not model. So a wrong or missing value passes all three gates. `INV-07` says pass criteria come from the specification, never from the generated firmware. Here they come from the generator's reading of the specification, not from an independent rule.
- **The good counter-example.** `motor::check_silicon` (`ir/src/motor.rs:298`) is an independent integer check on silicon's ARR+1. A mutant there was killed by two tests.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Recommendation.** One independent rule per generated register family in `aethelgard-ir`, in the manner of `check_silicon`: pin policy from the bus kind, `TIMINGR` from RM0433's formula, SPI `MBR` from `speed_hz`. B2's ledger covers what still has none.

## A8. The Fm+ pull-up floor assumes a 20 mA sink the firmware never enables; the Sm/Fm floor is taken at the nominal rail — medium, new

- **Evidence (read).**
  - `ir/src/electrical.rs:63-68` gives 20 mA for `FastPlus`. `solve.rs:615` passes `domain.nominal_millivolts`.
  - The generator admits `("FastPlus1M", 1_000_000, 0x0030_0B29)` (`svd-hal/src/board.rs:135`).
  - `rg SYSCFG crates/aethelgard-svd-hal/src` finds nothing. `rg 'FT_f'` over `solve.rs` finds nothing, so the pin's pad type is not checked (by search only).
- **Datasheet (read, DS12117 Rev 10, the I/O VOL rows and the I2C section).** `VOLFM+`, "for an FTf I/O pin in FM+ mode": 0.4 V at 20 mA. Otherwise 0.4 V at 8 mA (2.7–3.6 V). "The 20 mA output drive requirement in Fast-mode Plus is not supported … Rp(min) = (VDD−VOL(max))/IOL(max)".
- **Consequence.** At 3.3 V and 1 MHz `solve` accepts from 145 Ω. Without FM+ mode the MCU guarantees its low level only to about 363 Ω. At +5 % the 3 mA floor is 1022 Ω, not 967.
- **Confidence:** medium-high.
- **Recommendation.** Take the floor at the domain maximum. For Fm+, generate the `SYSCFG_PMCR` write and restrict to `FT_f` pins, or use 8 mA.

## A10. I2C `TIMINGR` values are literals nothing independent witnesses — low, partly recorded ("placeholders")

- **Evidence (read).** `svd-hal/src/board.rs:132-136`. The generated crate says "placeholders for a 64 MHz kernel clock". The oracle uses the same table (A5), and Renode ignores `TIMINGR`.
- **Arithmetic (mine).** At 15.625 ns per tick, before RM0433's synchronisation and filter delays: 400 k gives 2.34 µs, 1 M gives 844 ns, 100 k gives 9.84 µs. The delays lengthen each. These are probably the vendor tool's values and fine; I did not add the delays.
- **Confidence:** medium.
- **Recommendation.** Compute from RM0433's formula with an independent check. The 3302 sheet already measures the SCL period.

## A11. Reader leniencies in `extract` — low, new

- **Run.** The reference board with one `(width 0.2)` rewritten as `(width "0.2")` or `(width +0.2)`: `extract` prints `pass`. `check drc` on the same file is `ToolError … no report`, because KiCad 10.0.7 does not load it.
- **Read (`eda/src/sexpr.rs:378-398`).** One `-` is stripped, then Rust's integer parser accepts another sign: `--5` reads as +5 mm.
- **Subagent, unverified.** `(version …)` is never pinned. A copper layer's user name (`(0 "F.Cu" signal "TOP")`) passes `declared`, and `copper_layer` tests `ends_with(".Cu")`.
- **Confidence:** high for the run and the parser; the subagent items are unverified.
- **Recommendation.** Accept only `-?[0-9]+(\.[0-9]{1,6})?` as a bare atom. Pin the version. Refuse a fourth layer-row element. `RUL-03` asks the reader to tokenise as KiCad does.

## A15. Sandbox and security boundaries (brief item 5)

- **CI and publish (read by me).** All three image workflows give `packages: write` only to a `main`-push job. Actions are pinned by commit. The ArduPilot image pins its base by digest, apt packages by version, GCC by sha256, pip by hash and the source by commit. The recorded residuals (4400a A7, D-2, D-4) stand.
- **Bridge.** Nine `unsafe` sites in `bridge/src/ffi.rs`, as at the first review. Every workspace crate still forbids `unsafe`.
- **New untrusted-input readers (subagent).** No bypass found.
  - **A15.1 Quadratic scan in the pinout ELF reader — low (availability), new; confirmed by reading, not run.** `crates/aethelgard-pincheck/src/elf.rs:154-162` scans the string table to the next NUL once per symbol, with no bound on name length. A 16 MiB ELF with about 400,000 symbols pointing at one 8 MiB unterminated name costs about 3 × 10^12 byte steps. `check pinout` is an MCP tool, and `RUL-13` gives the call no timeout. Recommendation: compare only `SYMBOL.len() + 1` bytes.
  - **Subagent, unverified (hardening).** `build` and `prove` on a caller's crate run a path-dependency proc macro and honour the crate's `.cargo/config.toml` and `rust-toolchain.toml` inside the sandbox; the build-script refusal comes after cargo has run (`cli/src/firmware.rs:176`). Reach is bounded by the sandbox's read set; `verify` generates its own crate. Whether any Renode log call prints ELF-derived strings (a route to forge a witness line) was not settled. `hwdef.h`: one `#define` inside a dead `#if 0` is accepted (`pincheck/src/header.rs:39-46`). Below Landlock ABI 6, concurrent tools share uid 65534 and can signal each other (a `ToolError`, not a pass).
  - **Held (subagent).** The pinout reader requires the symbol's bytes to agree through both its section and the single `PT_LOAD`, with `vaddr == paddr`, so an ELF cannot show one table and load another. `verify` reads only its own binary's serialised JSON, binds each gate by hash and recomputes run ids. `uid_boundary` is printed only after the ids are read back. Past the 8 MiB log budget every witness becomes unwitnessed. The ArduPilot workflow's privileged job consumes no PR artifact.

# Part C: top three

1. **A day of hardware truth before more surface.** More than ten work orders merged on 2026-11-15 alone (`git log`), and four builders are running. Meanwhile the reference board cannot clock its SPI flash (A1), under-decouples its MCU and cannot be programmed (A2), declares a brown-out level nothing sets (A3), and would pass its power checks with nothing damping a battery hot-plug (A4). A1 fails in the first bench minute. A2, A3 and A4 pass the bench and bite later, in flight, so they deserve at least equal weight. Fix the four, then run the 3302 sheet that has been blank since the Nucleo arrived. It is the cheapest evidence available, and the first review already asked for it.

2. **Make unwitnessed silicon facts visible (A5, B2).** The proof's oracle and the code come from one generator, and the twin ignores the same fields, so whole register families pass three gates on the generator's word alone. The timer check shows the fix: one independent rule per family, and a ledger row in the verdict for anything that has none. That turns "pass" into "pass, and here is what only a bench can confirm". It would have caught A1, A8 and A10 without a reviewer.

3. **Judge the pair that flies, and mean "verified" at fabrication (A6, B4, B6).** For Sentinel-02 the dynamic gates judge bring-up firmware, while ArduPilot gets a static pin table, and on a motor board `verify` can write a package with its main proof skipped. Before `4500` orders anything: refuse the package while a harness is skipped, split that harness, and spike ArduPilot's boot and sensor probes in the twin. Keep `main` green while doing it (A7). A check that fails unread is the one failure this project's method cannot survive.
