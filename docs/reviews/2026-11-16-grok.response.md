Every claim in `2026-11-16-grok.md` was checked against the tree on 2026-11-16, from `main` at `8030fa5` and the open branches. The checking was done by two read-only subagents, plus the architect at source for the solver bug. Each verdict cites file:line.

The review read `main` before several of its "not in code" items landed or ran, so its status table is a snapshot. The thesis is accepted.

**The thesis.** A green `verify` today means the generator agrees with itself, and the twin agrees with the generator. That is `RUL-20`'s admission. The witness ledger (`AET-WO-4230`) and the support gate (`AET-WO-4300g`) are the work that turns consistency into judgement. Both are running, and both merge before anything new.

## 3. The strategy: what the architect adopts, and what is Scotty's

**Adopted now:**
- **Order.** The bench, then `4230` and `4300g`, before any new slice. Scotty's standing order tonight is backlog first, and every running builder is either one of those two or a backlog work order that takes no version number.
- **The I2C fix before any fabrication** (`1206` §1.7).
- **Split the god files on touch** (§6).
- **Delete the merged spikes.**
- **No third Renode model of our own; freeze the seccomp filter.** Residuals stay documented. `AET-WO-1603` adds no syscall rules: it touches the Landlock grant, the pre-flight, the log channel and `hwdef.h`.

**For Scotty, with the architect's recommendation:**
1. **Freeze the specification after `4300g` and `4230`** (specification 11 and 12, which are the review's own fixes) until the first scar is ratified into northstar §2. *Recommend yes.*
2. **Buy Sentinel-01 (the two-layer reference probe) as the first fabricated board** once `4230` and `4300g` are in and the scar is written, not Sentinel-02. It is cheap, and it falsifies the extractor, the router and the support gate before a four-layer flight controller does. *Recommend yes.* This changes the 2026-11-09 ruling that the first board bought is meant to be used.
3. **Hold `4400b`, `4400c` and `4500` until the scar** (the sensor rows). *Recommend yes; nothing of them is running.*
4. **The README's install line.** Say now that crates.io 0.1.0 is P1-era and the image digest is the supported interface, while 0.2.0 waits for its window. *Recommend yes.*
5. **The documents' size.** Move the §7 reconciliation history and the per-WO amendments to `docs/history/`, so `roadmap.md` and `architecture.md` read as a current plan and the current rulings. *Recommend a follow-up docs PR once tonight's merges settle.*
6. **The RP2350 earlier than "after a drone."** *Recommend after Sentinel-01's scar*, which the review itself says.

## 4. What the review gets wrong, briefly

- **The ground pour** fails closed (§2 row 3).
- **The off-board capacitance cap** is an overflow guard, not a judgement (row 8).
- **The power undercount** is mostly disclosed or conservative (row 9).
- **Byte identity** is per engine version (row 13).

"Four facts reach a fabrication package with every gate green" was true at its snapshot. One of the four (`4300d3`) has since merged, and the other three are the running work orders.
