About **80k lines of engine** and **61k lines of tests**, fail-closed JSON, byte-identical regeneration, a sandbox that was attacked until the holes had names. This is not a demo.

## A real solver bug

The I2C ceiling takes the **stricter** declared rise, then maps a smaller number onto a **faster** mode.

```746:768:crates/aethelgard-ir/src/solve.rs
/// Each I2C bus's ceiling, from the stricter of its two lines' declared
/// `max_rise_time_nanoseconds`, ...
t_max = t_max.min(c.max_rise_time_nanoseconds);
```

`ceiling_hz` says ≤120 ns is 1 MHz, ≤300 ns is 400 kHz, ≤1000 ns is 100 kHz. The bus is only legal for the mode the **worse** line can meet, so the input has to be `max`, not `min`. SDA at 1000 ns and SCL at 120 ns with `speed_hz` of 1 MHz is accepted. Both lines equal in the Sentinel fixture, so the suite never sees it.

Same file, the pull-up **maximum** from UM10204 (`tr / (0.8473 · Cb)`) is not implemented at all. The comment says that physics belongs to the twin. A 47 kΩ pull-up on a declared 400 pF clears the sink floor and is never checked against 300 ns or 1000 ns. The floor that does exist uses **nominal** millivolts, not the domain maximum, so at 3.3 V ± 5 % the legal resistor is smaller than the high rail requires.

## The ground pour can pass and then vanish

`route::connectivity` flood-fills "sure" zone nodes on a 125 µm grid. KiCad's zone is written **unfilled**, with `min_thickness` 0.25 mm and no `connect_pads`, so the default join is a thermal spoke. A one-node neck is connected in the flood and deleted by the refill. `island_removal_mode` 0 then drops the via. The only real fill is `kicad-cli pcb drc --refill-zones`, and that filled board is not what gets saved.

Do not treat a router `ok` as a poured ground. Before any fab, refill, treat unconnected items as fatal, and look at whether each ground via still sits in one pour.

## What else is load-bearing

- **Absence does not halt.** A sensor part with no table row is skipped. It still gets pins, a supply, and a bus, and no address, voltage, or clock check. `INV-06` holds for missing JSON keys and not for missing engineering data.
- **SPI mode is parsed and thrown away.** Mode 0 and mode 3 can share a controller.
- **Free chip-selects are placed after the search**, first leftover pin in key order. That prefers `PA0`, which is also the ADC sense pin. Sense then fails `NoAdcPin` with no backtrack.
- **`SolvedWire` fabricates missing fields** (`i2c_speeds`, `clock`, `power`, …) when the schema version is not 7, including `SolvedClock::reset()`. `validate` rejects the version later. A caller that skips it reads a made-up topology.
- **Off-board capacitance** is capped at 1 µF specifically "so no real line is refused." A connector can declare 30 pF of cable on top of a 400 pF net and both gates pass.
- **Power accounting undercounts.** A translator's supply current is not counted, and a bad `i64` load is swallowed as zero. The buck's input is taken as its output current, which overestimates a step-down. The first two do not.
- **The flash symbol is the wrong part.** `W25Q64` is drawn as `Memory_Flash:W25Q32JVSS` with the value rewritten. Footprint follows the Q32. That is a purchasing error with a green ERC.
- **Extraction is a real upper bound on bare copper, and the verdict overclaims it.** No resistance, no inductance, no zone copper, no part bodies. Every I2C pin is a flat 10 pF, MCU included. The CLI still says "upper bound estimated from routed geometry."
- **`strict.rs` fails open** on a KiCad child it does not have a rule for. A new alias that overwrites a thickness is invisible to the gate and live in KiCad.
- **Byte-identical across builds is false.** Schematic UUIDs are content-hashed, but `generator_version` is `CARGO_PKG_VERSION`.

`hse_hz` can only be 0. `SolvedClock` accepts only `reset()`. SPI `modes`, the dead DS-009 ports, and the second `Lifecycle` enum are subtraction candidates, not features.

Nothing here moves the order from the first note. Bench, then `4230` and `4300g`. Add one item in front of fabrication and nowhere else: fix the rise-time `min`, and do not trust `route::connectivity` until a refilled pour has been diffed against it.
