- **R6 · The drill check** [read]: `eda/src/fab.rs` `holes` counts each tool's hits; `Drill` holds no positions.
- **R7 · The straps** [read]: `schematic.rs:733-775` maps BME280 0x76/0x77 and TMP117 0x48–0x4B to the strap's connection, and keeps an `allowed` text per part; `parts.json` lists the same addresses in the same order. `validate` already refuses an address the part's row does not offer (`I2cAddressNotOffered`, `ir/src/lib.rs:2657`).

## 4. The seeded neck, against KiCad's refill [measured, pinned image]

A 20 × 10 mm board whose B.Cu ground zone joins its two ground vias only through a neck at y = 105 mm, between two U's of `/A` copper (B.Cu, x = 110–111 mm, closed to each edge by vias); the neck's copper-to-copper gap g is swept. `route::connectivity` (the router's and `extract`'s zone judge, on `zone_core`) and `check drc` (KiCad 10.0.7, `--refill-zones`):

| g (µm) | the judge | KiCad's refill |
|---|---|---|
| 600, 640 | cut ("the zone does not reach via") | cut: `unconnected_items`, exit 1 |
| 650, 660 | cut | joined: pass |
| 670 | joined, through one grid node | joined |
| 680, 800 | joined | joined |

The judge's threshold: a core node needs clearance 0.2 + min_thickness/2 0.125 + 10 µm = 0.335 mm to each wall's copper, so 2 × 0.335 = 670 µm with the node on a grid row. KiCad's: 2 × 0.2 + 0.25 = 650 µm. **The judge never joins what KiCad cuts**: at 670 µm, exactly the review's "one-node neck", KiCad keeps a 0.27 mm sliver (≥ min_thickness). Between 650 and 660 µm the judge fails closed. P9 was wrong in one part: one step narrower than the judge's threshold, KiCad still joins (only at ≤ 640 do both cut). Pinned as `with_tools::a_seeded_neck_is_joined_by_kicads_refill_wherever_the_judge_joins_it` (7 DRC runs, about 4.6 s in the image; no weight row, since `ci/test-weights.tsv` holds CI-measured times only). TIER2_MIN 157 → 158; `4230`'s inbox told.
