> **What this is** (`1211` §5 item 1, as ruled):
> - **A top-level `pin_devices` list** (Q1 shape A), private, read through `Specification::declared_pin_devices` (`RUL-16`). Each entry has a `reference`, a `part` whose row has `bus: none`, `supplies`, `straps`, `signals` (GPIO), `analog` (ADC) and `bridges`, with the refusals in `1211` §2.1–2.5.
> - **Parts schema 11:**
>   - `bus: none` and `identity: null` for a part with no bus;
>   - per pin: `kind`, its internal pull, and its levels;
>   - the part's timing and per-mode truth tables, with `drives` and a `forbidden` set (Q3, Q6).
>   - Two rows: DRV8876PWPR and DRV8833PWPR, every value cited to the held maker's document. **A value no held document states is not entered** (`1211` §7's NOT FOUND table): a check that needs one refuses by name (`PartLimitUndeclared`), or the specification declares it with a citation (`RUL-14`).
> - **`pwm.idle`** (`level` low or high, or `pulse_us` > 0; a `pulse_us` of 0 is `IdleSpelledTwice`, Q4) and **`pwm.resolution_ps`**, both required on every PWM output with no default (`INV-06`, Q5). `resolution_ps` joins the timer-share key (`TimerResolutionConflict`).
> - **The bridge rule** (Q3): a bridge input idles at a `level` (`BridgeIdlePulsed`), and the static idle tuple must be a non-driving row of the part's truth table in its strapped mode (`BridgeIdleDrives`). It judges the idle only.
> - **The solver:**
>   - `topology.gpio` becomes `{net: {pin, direction, initial | pull}}`;
>   - `adc_inputs: {net: {pin, peripheral, channel}}` prefers ADC1/ADC3, and an ADC2-only pin is `AdcUnmodelled` unless pinned (Q8);
>   - `power.sense` is solved into the same map and stays in the power tree;
>   - `motor_output_pupd` follows the idle (Q9);
>   - `check_silicon` judges the idle words.
>   - The solved version is the next after `4240a`'s 13.
> - **Every other reader of the new shapes refuses a pin device by name,** on `HseNotGenerated`'s precedent: `PinDeviceNotGenerated` (svd-hal, cli `generate`/`emit`/`simulate`), `PinDeviceNotSimulated` (twin), `PinDeviceNotDrawn` (eda) and `GpioNotEmitted` (hwdef). `pincheck` reads the directional `gpio` and still refuses what it cannot judge.
>
> It is NOT:
> - the generator, Kani or ledger (`1211b`);
> - the twin, chaos or verify (`1211c`);
> - the eda arms (`1211d`);
> - a change to any solved word, emitted byte or ELF of a specification that declares no pin device (beyond the version and hash lines).

3. **The solver:** the directional `gpio`, `adc_inputs` with a chosen peripheral, the timer key, and idle CCR words (0, ARR + 1, or the pulse). `ccr + 1 >= ticks` stays for commanded pulses and is lifted for the idle word alone (`1211` §2.4).    - `schema_version` on every committed specification;

7. **Mutants:**
   - accept a pulsed bridge idle: a test fails;

   - let the solver choose ADC2: a test fails.

## 5. Adversarial pass, full

- **A driver declaration that validates and would drive the motor at boot:** a pulsed idle on a bridge, an nSLEEP high with a driving input tuple, or a strap that selects another mode.
- **An ADC input that passes `AdcFullScaleExceeded` at typical gain but fails at the band edge.**
- **A specification whose solved words moved** through the migration.
- **A reader of `topology.gpio`** that silently mis-reads the new shape instead of refusing.

## 7. Flag-and-stop conditions

- **A solved word of an existing specification moves.** Stop with the diff.
- **A check needs a value no held maker's document states,** and the specification cannot declare it with a citation. Stop and name it.
- **The named-refusal fence cannot hold:** a reader of the new shape needs a real feature rather than a refusal. Stop with the case.
