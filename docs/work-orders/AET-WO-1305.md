# AET-WO-1305 — Extraction, first step: an upper bound on each I2C line's capacitance from the routed board, judged against the declared bound

**Repo:** `ClearForge-LLC/aethelgard` · **Base:** `main` after `AET-WO-1601`, named in the kickoff.
**Branch:** `wo/aet-1305-extract-bound`.
**Author:** Claude (architect) · **Builder:** strong tier, with a full adversarial pass (`docs/roadmap.md` §6). An estimate that reads as more precise than it is would be a confident wrong answer.
**Phase:** P3. It **gates `AET-WO-3300`**, alongside `AET-WO-1601`. The Mythos review's B3, first step; the full `AET-WO-1303` stays before `4500`.

**Grounds:**
- **`RUL-19` (1):** the campaign's rise time is computed from the specification's declared pull-up and its declared `max_capacitance_femtofarads` bound. No property of the routed board reaches it (review A1).
- **Why a bound is enough for P3.** RC rise time grows monotonically with C. If the routed board's capacitance on a line is at most the declared bound, then the campaign, which simulated that bound, has judged a rise at least as slow as the board's. Its pass carries over to the board for capacitance. That argument needs a true upper bound, not a typical value, so every term below is a bound with a cited source.
- **What already exists:**
  - `aethelgard_eda::route::read` reads a `.kicad_pcb` into `Board` and `Routed`, with segments, vias and zones;
  - `route::connectivity` refuses a board whose net labels disagree with its copper. `AET-WO-1303` names these two as extraction's first step;
  - the router's geometry is fixed by constants (`WIDTH_NM`, `CLEARANCE_NM`, `VIA_NM`, `DRILL_NM`), and the emitted board is 1.6 mm thick.

> **What this is:** a new gate that reads the routed board, bounds each I2C line's capacitance from its own copper, and judges the bound against the line's declared bound.
>
> It is NOT:
> - feeding extracted values into the campaign;
> - a field solver;
> - extraction of resistance or inductance;
> - the full `AET-WO-1303`.

**Cadence:** build-only, with one measurement step (§1.3). Stop on §7. One PR, left unmerged.

## 1. Scope

### 1.1 `aethelgard extract --board <pcb> --spec <spec>`

- **The board's nets come from its copper.** Read the board with `route::read` and run `route::connectivity` first. A board whose labels disagree with its copper is refused with a named error before any estimate.
- **For each I2C line of the specification** (each bus's SDA and SCL net, after `solve`), compute an upper bound `C_bound` as the sum of four terms:
  - **Trace:** the line's routed length (`Routed`'s Manhattan length, itself at least the Euclidean length) times a per-length bound. That bound is the capacitance of a grounded coplanar trace with the router's `WIDTH_NM`, copper at `CLEARANCE_NM` on both sides and a ground plane under the emitted board's dielectric. This is the most capacitive geometry this stackup and these rules allow, so it bounds any real neighbourhood of the trace. Take the closed form from a cited reference (for example Wadell, *Transmission Line Design Handbook*), with relative permittivity at the top of FR-4's range (state the value and its source).
  - **Vias:** for each via on the net, a cited closed form for via capacitance at the router's `VIA_NM` and `DRILL_NM`, with an antipad at `CLEARANCE_NM`.
  - **Pads:** each copper pad on the net that is not an I2C device pin (a pull-up resistor's pad, for example). Use a parallel-plate bound over the board's dielectric with a stated fringing factor, and cite the factor.
  - **Pins:** each I2C device pin on the net, the MCU's included, at UM10204's maximum input capacitance per pin (rev. 7, table 10, `Ci`: confirm the value from the document). A part's datasheet maximum may replace it only where the datasheet states a *maximum*, never a typical value, with the section cited.
- **Integer femtofarads throughout.** Rounding is upward at every step, so the result stays a bound.
- **The verdict** is one JSON line (`RUL-10`):
  - the board's and the specification's sha256;
  - for each line, `C_bound` and its four terms, the declared bound, and `pass` or `fail`;
  - `"capacitance": "upper bound estimated from routed geometry"` (`RUL-19`).
- **Exit codes:** 0 when every line's `C_bound` is at most its declared bound; 1 (a design fail) naming each line over its bound; 2 or 3 for refusals, as for every subcommand.

### 1.2 `verify` runs it

`aethelgard verify` runs `extract` as a gate after DRC. It binds the board's and the specification's sha256 to the generated bytes, as every gate is bound (`AET-WO-3301`'s binding table).

### 1.3 Measure on the reference, before any acceptance number is written

Record in FEEDBACK, for each reference I2C line:
- every term;
- the total;
- the declared bound (100 pF).

Then predict and record the estimate for a line whose declared bound would be exceeded. For example, a specification declaring 20 pF on the reference board should fail on its pins alone. If the reference itself fails its declared bound, that is a §7 stop: the answer goes to the architect, not into the code.

### 1.4 The constants live in one place

Every constant in §1.1 lives in one module of `aethelgard-eda`, beside the router's geometry, each with its source in a doc comment:
- the per-length bound;
- the via and pad bounds;
- the permittivity;
- the fringing factor;
- the pin bound.

A test pins each derived constant against its formula, so a change to a router constant re-derives it, or fails loudly.

## 3. Acceptance

- **The reference board** (`aethelgard generate` on `examples/sentinel-01-minimal.json`) passes `extract`. Every term is recorded (§1.3).
- **The same board against a specification** whose declared bound is below the measured `C_bound` fails, exit 1, naming each line and its numbers.
- **A board whose labels disagree with its copper** is refused before any estimate.
- **A longer route raises the bound:** a hand-edited board with one I2C segment doubled in length raises that line's trace term by the per-length bound times the added length, to the femtofarad.
- **`verify`** on the reference passes with `extract` among its gates, and binds its hashes. A swapped board in `verify`'s scratch is a `BindingMismatch`.
- **The gates** (`CLAUDE.md`) pass. So do `gates`, `tools` and `image` in CI.

## 5. Adversarial pass — full

1. **Find a geometry the router can produce whose real capacitance exceeds `C_bound`:** a neighbour closer than `CLEARANCE_NM`, a zone on the same layer, a stub or a via-in-pad. Each one that works is a finding with a fix, or a reason the router cannot produce it.
2. **Make `extract` pass a line it should fail:** a net named twice, a segment with no net, a pad left out, overflow, or rounding.
3. **Find wording in the verdict or the README that claims more than a bound.**

## 7. Flag-and-stop conditions

- The reference board's `C_bound` exceeds its declared bound on any line.
- No citable closed form bounds the trace or via term for this stackup.
- A datasheet's typical value is the only figure available for a pin, and UM10204's bound cannot be used.
