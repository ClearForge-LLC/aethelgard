# AET-WO-1313 — A declared board outline: the placer packs a board inside it, or refuses by name

**Repo:** `ClearForge-LLC/aethelgard` · **Base:** `main`, named in the kickoff.
**Branch:** `wo/aet-1313-outline`.
**Author:** Claude (architect) · **Builder:** strong tier, with a full adversarial pass.
**Phase:** P1 board capability, for Sentinel-03 (roadmap §5, the Sentinel ruling of 2026-11-17). Written 2026-11-17. **Cadence: spike, then build, with one measured stop (§1.5).**

**Grounds:**
- **Scotty, 2026-11-17.** Sentinel-03 keeps the flight sensors and is cost-constrained. He gets the fabrication quote after this work order, so the quote prices a board that fits.
- **`AET-WO-3400` FEEDBACK (on `main`):**
  - The loaded variants come out at **100.05 × 107.60 mm** (V5, 68 parts; V6, 71 parts) and **100.05 × 118.95 mm** (V7, 79 parts). Each routes and passes every gate it was run through, at two and at four layers.
  - **The outline is the same at four layers** [measured]: the placer does not read the layer count, so four layers' extra routing room buys no area.
  - §10.4 prices the paths. OSH Park prices by area, and a fabricator's price break at 100 × 100 mm sits just under V5's outline.
  - The variants are generated by `spike/3400/make_variants.py`. `AET-WO-3401` deletes `spike/3400/` from the tree, but it stays readable at `2b43672`.
- **The placer (`aethelgard-eda/src/pcb.rs:494-512`, `AET-WO-1301` §1.2)** is greedy and top-side only:
  - each part goes at the first free spot, in rings around the part it shares the most nets with;
  - it keeps fixed gaps (`GAP_NM` 0.5 mm, `IC_GAP_NM` 1 mm) and a 2 mm margin (`MARGIN_NM`);
  - connectors go along the bottom edge last;
  - the outline is the bounding box of whatever results.

  **Nothing in it tries to make that box small.**
- **The backlog row from `1307` K3:** placement orders by reference, so renaming references fails some boards. Its owner is "the next placement work order". This is that work order, for measurement (§1.4); fixing it is not required.

> **What this is:** a specification-declared bound on the board outline. The placer packs every part inside it, deterministically, or refuses by name with the best outline it found. On a board that declares no bound, nothing changes.
>
> It is NOT:
> - a new router;
> - USB, the witness's capture port, or the hot-plug draw (their own work orders);
> - any change to an existing board's bytes;
> - parts on the bottom side, unless the spike shows top-side cannot fit and Scotty rules it in (§1.5): it changes assembly cost and his hand assembly.

## 2. Build, after the ruling

1. **`board.max_outline_mm: { "width": W, "height": H }`.** Optional, under the precedent ruled on #191 (Q1): its absence is the conservative case, today's placer, and is judged as such.
   - `validate` refuses a bound that cannot hold the parts' keep-out boxes at all, by name, before any placement. This is a cheap area check.
   - The placer packs inside the bound by the ruled method, or refuses with `OutlineUnreachable`, naming the best outline it reached and the shortfall.
2. **Determinism:** the same specification gives the same bytes, run after run and machine after machine (`INV-07`'s regeneration).
3. **The fixture passes every gate at four layers** inside 100 × 100 mm, or the work order stops (§7).
4. **Versions:** the specification takes its next number (15, after `1312`'s 14), with the refusal by name for older readers that earlier bumps had. No other version moves.

## 5. Adversarial pass, full

- **A board that fits only because a part left the board,** or sits over the edge, or overlaps another.
- **A placement that passes DRC but breaks parity,** or moves a decoupling capacitor away from the pin it decouples.
- **Nondeterminism:** the same specification emitted twice, and on two machines. Any byte that differs fails.
- **A bound just above the keep-out sum,** which `validate` accepts and the placer cannot meet: refused by name, never a partial board.
- **A connector moved off the edge** its cable needs.
