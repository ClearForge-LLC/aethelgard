# AET-WO-1324 — The router's next two: vias whose junction regions meet on a supply net, and a work count that bounds wall time on one huge net

**Repo:** `ClearForge-LLC/aethelgard` · **Base:** `main`, named in the kickoff.
**Branch:** `wo/aet-1324-router-vias-and-scan`.
**Author:** Claude (architect) · **Builder:** strong tier, with a full adversarial pass.
**Phase:** P4, backlog. Written 2026-11-24. **Cadence:** a measurement, then a build with one stop. **After `4410`** (its PC0 sense on `sentinel-02-power` is what exposes (1)).

**Grounds** (roadmap §7.1, from `main`):
- **`4410` Q12** (FEEDBACK §6): with the sense on PC0, `sentinel-02-power` on two layers routes two vias whose junction regions meet. `extract` refuses this with `ViaRegionsOverlap` at (93,250,000, 87,125,000) nm, where on the old sense it refused `GroundReturnUnread`. The refusal is `crates/aethelgard-eda/src/supply.rs` (the junction-region loop, about L700–715); the test is `cli/tests/supply_tier1.rs` L499. Both fail closed, so the board is refused either way.
- **`1323` D6** (FEEDBACK A2, §10): the work count is not a runtime guard on every board. One unstitched net of 1,600 pads did 348,036 units (0.1 % of `WORK`) in 130.2 s; 900 pads took 20.9 s and 400 pads 1.9 s. That is about pad count squared, from work outside the count: `route_net`'s nearest-pad scan (each remaining pad against every tree node), searches seeded from the whole tree, and the final judges.
- **`1307` A4** and **`1323` (1):** the work bound refuses by name. This work order makes it hold for wall time too.

> **What this is:**
> - **(1) The meeting junction regions.** Measure first:
>   - why the router places two vias on that supply net so close together;
>   - whether two vias that close are a fabrication fault, or only something the supply model cannot represent. Cite the junction model's own source (`1303`/`1303b`'s derivation).
>   - **Stop** with the measurement and one of:
>     - (a) the router keeps a supply net's vias at least one junction region apart, as a routing rule from the same constant `extract` uses;
>     - (b) `extract` merges meeting regions into one region with its own certified solve;
>     - (c) another option you find.
>
>   The architect's prior is (a), since it keeps the certified model unchanged. Then `sentinel-02-power` either extracts, or is refused by the next gate by name, with that name recorded.
> - **(2) The scan inside the count.** Measure each candidate's share of the 130 s. Then either:
>   - charge the scan and the whole-tree seeds to `WORK`, so the existing bound refuses by name before wall time grows; or
>   - index the tree, so the scan is no longer quadratic.
>
>   Indexing is preferred if it leaves every committed board's bytes unchanged, which needs the nearest-pad choice and its tie-break preserved, proven by the goldens. Charging the count is the fallback, with `WORK`'s headroom on every committed board stated.
>   - **The scaling test** uses counts, not wall time: the work charged for 400, 900 and 1,600 pads, against a bound the test states. A wall-time figure goes in FEEDBACK only.
> - **Each refusal or rule comes with a test, and each test with a mutant it kills.**
>
> It is NOT:
> - the placer (W4's), capacitance-aware placement, or `fab_tolerances`' vias;
> - `4300j` R5/R6 (support step 8's resistor count, an eda rules work order);
> - any change to the supply model's certified solve, unless (1) is ruled (b).

## 5. Adversarial pass, full

- **Two vias that still meet:** on another layer pair, a via-in-pad, or a rip-up that moves one via back.

- **A count that undercounts:** a seeded search, or a judge run outside `route_net`.
