# aethelgard-pincheck

The pinout check (`AET-WO-4400a`, ruling D7 on PR #130). It judges what a linked STM32H753 firmware writes to its GPIO registers at reset against the `SolvedTopology` the firmware was meant to realise. The CLI runs it as `aethelgard check pinout` (`crates/aethelgard-cli/README.md`).

**What it reads.**
- **The ELF.** `gpio_default_config`, found in `.symtab` by name, type and size: exactly one `STT_OBJECT` of 308 bytes, which is 11 ports × `MODER OTYPER OSPEEDR PUPDR ODR AFRL AFRH`. ChibiOS's `gpio_init` writes these bytes at reset.
  - The bytes are located through the symbol's section and through the `PT_LOAD` that holds them, and the two must agree.
  - The segment must run where it is flashed (`p_vaddr == p_paddr`), no other segment may flash over the bytes, and the section may not be writable.
  - The reader is a few hundred lines of strict ELF32 little-endian parsing with no dependency; no toolchain runs.
- **`hwdef.h`, optional.** ArduPilot's `VAL_GPIOx_MODER/AFRL/AFRH`. Each must equal the ELF's decode, or the check refuses (exit 5). A disagreement is never a pass.
- **The topology, the package and the allow-list.** These give the expected values. The package is the solver's pin table, passed in by the caller. The allow-list is a declared file of pins outside the topology, each with its mode, its AF and why. The check holds no list of its own and never reads a `hwdef.dat` (`INV-07`).

**What it judges.**
- **forward:** every assignment's pin is alternate at the solved AF, and every `gpio` pin is an output. Since `AET-WO-4400b` (§1.3), every topology pin's drive is also judged from the same bytes:
  - **`OSPEEDR`** against the speed `solve` stored in `pin_speeds` (`WrongSpeed`);
  - **`OTYPER`** open-drain exactly on the I2C lines, `aethelgard_ir::silicon::implied_open_drain` (`WrongOutputType`);
  - **`PUPDR`** against `aethelgard_ir::silicon::implied_pupd`: a pin device's GPIO input's declared pull; a motor output's by its declared fitment (`AET-WO-4300j` D6): `fitted`, none, `00`, whose pull-down is the declared external resistor (`AET-WO-4300i` D0), `open`, the internal pull-down, `10`; no internal pull on every other pin (`WrongPull`).

  The check reads the stored speed and calls these two rules. The generator calls neither, and stores nothing (`RUL-20`); a test in `aethelgard-hwdef` holds that. A topology with no stored speed for a pin is refused (`NoPinSpeed`), never judged as `00`.
  - **`ODR`** of every `gpio` output against its solved level, `initial` (`WrongOutputLevel`, `AET-WO-4410` (4), review 3 A12): a chip select is `high`, so an image that drives it low from its first write is named.
- **debug:** PA13 and PA14, the SWD port, are each at RM0433's reset state, alternate AF0, from the check's own reset table (`SWD`; `SwdNotKept`, `AET-WO-4410` (3), review 3 A11). A topology net or an allow-list entry on either is refused (`SwdPinNamed`, `AllowNamesSwdPin`).
- **reverse:** every *configured* pin is named by the topology or the allow-list. An allowed pin is exactly as declared, and the allow-list may not name a topology pin.
- **package:** every configured pin exists in the package.

A pin is **configured** when it is output or alternate and not at its reset state. Reset is RM0433 Rev 9 §11.4.1: `MODER` is analog except PA13–PA15 and PB3–PB4, which are alternate. §11.4.9–10 put both AF registers at 0. Input and analog drive nothing, and ArduPilot writes input to every pin it is not told about.

The exception is a `DEFAULTGPIO` line before the `MCU` line, which sets every pin of ports A–K. With `OUTPUT`, the package check then names every pin the LQFP144 does not bond out, and no allow-list can clear them (`AET-WO-4400a` FEEDBACK, decision for `4400b`).

**Order** (`AET-WO-4410` (6), `1218` F4). Every refusal the topology and the package alone decide (`validate`, `UnsupportedPart`, `PartMismatch`, `GpioNotJudged`, `InvalidPin`, `NoPinSpeed`, `SwdPinNamed`) runs before the ELF, `hwdef.h` or the allow-list is read (`admit`), so a topology refusal is never hidden behind an unreadable image.

**A function-like `#define`** of any name the reader reads, `NAME(` with no blank before the `(`, is refused by name (`MalformedHeader`, `AET-WO-4410` (5), review 3 A15), never read as "not defined". A blank or a comment before `(` is object-like, as GCC reads it; `%:define` and a continuation before `(` are not escapes.

**`SYSCFG_PMCR`, a cited source fact** (`pmcr`, `AET-WO-4410` D1). LQFP144 pads 28 and 29 are `PC2_C` and `PC3_C` only while PC2SO and PC3SO hold their reset value (RM0433 Rev 9 p. 549). No image this check reads holds `PMCR`. The one writer in ArduPilot `fb773567` and ChibiOS `d103983f` is `mac_lld_init` (`hal_mac_lld.c:306`, `:308`, `:310`), and it masks only PA1SO and EPIS; `pmcr::WRITERS` holds each, and a test names the fact. It is re-read at any ArduPilot re-pin. On a running image the twin's access log owns it (a Tviburi row, `SYSCFG_PMCR`).

**The identity judge** (`AET-WO-4400b2`, `src/identity.rs`). When `hwdef.h` is given, it also judges the facts ArduPilot keeps in its device tables rather than in `gpio_default_config`. Each is read from ArduPilot's own computation and held against the topology; the generated `hwdef.dat` is never read. Its findings are `check: "identity"`:
- **Each SPI device the parts table names for ArduPilot,** from `HAL_SPI_DEVICE_LIST` and `HAL_SPI_BUS_LIST` in `hwdef.h`. The table is built by a constructor at start-up, so the ELF holds no bytes of it. The judge holds one `SPIDesc` of the part's `spidev` name on its solved controller, its chip select on its solved pin, the driven mode, and speeds within the kHz at or below the controller's `achieved_hz`. Findings: `MissingSpiDevice`, `SpiDeviceTwice`, `UnknownSpiDevice`, `WrongSpiBus`, `WrongChipSelect`, `WrongSpiMode`, `WrongSpiSpeed`.
- **Each motor output,** from each `HAL_PWM_GROUPS` entry's channel map and `&PWMDt`, also constructor-built. The motor's `PWM(n)` index must sit on its solved timer channel, and every other channel must be disabled. Findings: `WrongMotorChannel`, `MissingPwmGroup`, `UnexpectedPwmOutput`.
- **The I2C clock,** `HAL_I2C_MAX_CLOCK`, or ArduPilot's 100 kHz default. It must not be above the slowest declared bus (`I2cClockAboveDeclared`), and ArduPilot's H7 timings must not be redefined (`I2cTimingOverridden`). `hwdef.h` holds the generated `define` verbatim, so the expected value is the topology's, never the file's.
- **The serial order and pins,** from the ELF's `_serial_tab`: `const` bytes in flash, 44-byte `SerialDef`s. SERIAL0 must be the console, then the specification's order, each on its solved U(S)ART and pins, and every later entry empty. Findings: `MissingSerialPort`, `WrongSerialPort`, `UnexpectedSerialPort`.
- **Each port's protocol, options and baud** (`AET-WO-4404`; from the ELF since `AET-WO-4405`), from `AP_SerialManager::var_info` in the ELF: the parameter table, `const` in flash, one 16-byte `AP_Param::GroupInfo` per compiled `SERIALn_*` row, each `#ifndef` default, the SERIAL7/8 literal options and `HAL_HAVE_SERIALn_PARAMS` already folded in by the compiler. Each is held against the port's role and declared inversion. The judge shares no code with the generator.
  - **The reader** (`elf::serial_params`) reads whole entries up to the first of type `AP_PARAM_NONE`, where ArduPilot stops (`AP_Param.cpp:1498-1500`). That entry must be exactly `AP_GROUPEND` and the table's last. Every other row must be `n_PROTOCOL` (`AP_Int8`), `n_BAUD` or `n_OPTIONS` (`AP_Int32`), or a pass-through row. It must carry no flags, so its default is a float, and the default must be one its type holds exactly. Each name is read where it is flashed. Each port row must sit at its member's offset, `24n` for `n_BAUD`, `24n + 4` for `n_OPTIONS`, `24n + 8` for `n_PROTOCOL`, because ArduPilot applies a default by offset whatever the name says (`AP_Param.cpp:1490-1497`; measured on every board, `spike/4406` §2.3). A missing symbol, a partial entry, no terminator, anything after it, a name outside the flashed image or longer than 16 bytes, a row named twice or unknown, a row at another offset (`VarInfoRowOffset`), and a port missing one of its rows are each refused by name.
  - **A declared port with no rows** (its `HAL_HAVE_SERIALn_PARAMS` is not 1, or it is past SERIAL9) is named: `MissingSerialParameters`.
  - **Protocol:** `n_PROTOCOL` must be exactly the role's: MAVLink 2 for `console` and `mavlink`, GPS for `gps`, RCIN for `rc_crsf` and `rc_sbus`. MAVLink 1 is a different wire, so `protocol_match`'s equivalences do not apply. SERIAL0's row is a literal MAVLink 2 (`:180`), so with no console, any other role standing there is named.
  - **Options:** `n_OPTIONS`. A port that is not RC input must set RXINV and TXINV exactly when it declares `inverted`, and no other bit: SWAP, half-duplex, the pulls, DMA and FIFO all change the wire or the pins. On RC input, RXINV is AP_RCProtocol's and is not judged; every other bit must be clear. SERIAL0 has no options row, so a port there declared inverted is named.
  - **Baud:** a non-RC port's `n_BAUD`, mapped as `map_baudrate` maps it (`:737-770`). `0_BAUD` is the define / 1000 (`:172`), and a port with no baud define holds its `#ifndef` default, also in kbaud. Nothing serial is read from `hwdef.h`. SERIAL0's first open, at `DEFAULT_SERIAL0_BAUD` itself (`:419`) before the GCS reopens it at `0_BAUD`, is not judged: `var_info` holds that rate only divided by 1000, so a define of 115999 shares 115200's row (`AET-WO-4405` D1).
  - **At most one compiled port may be RC input**, since ArduPilot drops the second (`:536-541`). **At most one GPS port per receiver** (or GPS2, which ArduPilot finds as GPS): `AP_GPS::init` gives each receiver one port at most (`AP_GPS.cpp:345-349`), and the receivers are read from the ELF (`AET-WO-4406`, `elf::gps_receivers`): one `AP_SUBGROUPINFO` row, `1_`, `2_`, …, per receiver in `AP_GPS::var_info` (`AP_GPS.cpp:280-286`; two on every measured board, one where `GPS_MAX_RECEIVERS` is 1). A group row that is not the next receiver's is refused (`MalformedGpsVarInfo`). With no `AP_GPS::var_info` (AP_GPS compiled out, which does not build Copter at this tag), each port the topology declares a GPS is named (`GpsNotCompiled`). At compiled defaults only the first is given one: `GPS2_TYPE` defaults to 0, which needs no UART (`AP_GPS_Params.cpp:31`, `AP_GPS.cpp:306-321`, `:328`), so a second GPS port waits for the user to set it. Both rules count every compiled port, declared or not, as ArduPilot's own search does.
  - **Findings:** `WrongSerialProtocol`, `WrongSerialOptions`, `WrongSerialBaud`, `RcInputTwice`, `GpsPortUnbound`, `GpsNotCompiled`, `MissingSerialParameters`.
- **The barometer probes,** from `HAL_BARO_PROBE_LIST` in order. Each must be the part's ArduPilot driver, on its solved controller (through `HAL_I2C_DEVICE_LIST`), at its address. Findings: `WrongBaroProbe`, `MissingBaroProbe`, `UnexpectedBaroProbe`.
- **The IMU probes** (`AET-WO-4400d`), from `HAL_INS_PROBE_LIST` in order (`AP_InertialSensor.cpp:1220-1222`). Each must be `ADD_BACKEND(AP_InertialSensor_<driver>::probe(*this,hal.spi->get_device("<name>"),ROTATION_<x>))` for an SPI device whose part's ArduPilot row is an IMU, in index order: the row's driver and `SPIDEV` name, and a rotation whose matrix is the declared orientation's. The judge holds its own table of ArduPilot's 26 right-angle `Rotation` names and their matrices (`vector3.cpp` at the tag), so either name of one rotation passes (ruled D4); it computes the expected matrix from the solved `orientation` with the board frame x forward, y right, z down through F.Cu (ruled D3), and shares no code with the generator. An IMU with no declared orientation, or whose driver frame is uncited, has no right rotation. The ELF must define the driver's `probe(AP_InertialSensor&, OwnPtr<Device>, Rotation)` under its mangled name (measured in the built ELF). A rotation spelled any other way, or redefined as a macro, is refused. A defined `HAL_SITL_INVENSENSEV3`, which compiles one more probe after the list (`AP_InertialSensor.cpp:1223-1225`), is named. Findings: `WrongImuProbe`, `WrongImuRotation`, `MissingImuProbe`, `UnexpectedImuProbe`, `ImuDriverNotLinked`.

Each table is read through the list macro the compiler expands, with any macro it names expanded in turn. A directive is matched with any blanks GCC allows and with `#` spelled `#` or `%:`; a name the judge reads that a compiled `#undef` or `#pragma` (`push_macro`, `pop_macro`) also names is refused, because it could stand another definition in (`AET-WO-4400d` adversarial); and an integer is read as C reads it (`0x` hex, a leading `0` octal). A serial entry's driver pointer must be its `SD<instance>`, and its padding zero. A table in a form the reader does not know is refused by macro name (`MalformedHeader`), never read loosely.

- **The battery monitor** (`AET-WO-4410` (1)), from `hwdef.h`, which holds each `define` verbatim (`chibios_hwdef.py:1007-1010`), and the analog table ArduPilot computes (`:2186-2266`), against the topology's solved sense by the judge's own arithmetic: `HAL_USE_ADC` not `FALSE` (`AdcOff`); `HAL_BATT_VOLT_PIN` the sense's ADC1 channel (`WrongBattVoltPin`; a sense on another ADC is `SenseNotOnAdc1`); `HAL_BATT_VOLT_SCALE` equal to `(top + bottom) / bottom` as an exact fraction, so a decimal that rounds it is named (`WrongBattVoltScale`); `HAL_BATT_MONITOR_DEFAULT` 3, voltage only (`WrongBattMonitorType`); and `HAL_ANALOG_PINS`' row for the channel with no multiplier, so the divider is applied once (`WrongAnalogPin`). With no sense solved, a `HAL_BATT_VOLT_PIN` is `UnexpectedBattMonitor`. The sense pin itself must decode as analog (forward, `NotAnalog`), and the allow-list may not name it.

**What the identity judge cannot see** (`AET-WO-4400b2` FEEDBACK §5):
- **The serial table, the serial parameters and the IMU driver's probe symbol are read from the ELF.** The other facts are read from `hwdef.h` alone, so `check pinout` judges an ELF and the `hwdef.h` of the same build, as CI gives them (D1).
- **The serial parameters are the compiled defaults, so an ELF that can replace them at boot is refused** (`ParamDefaultsOverride`, `AET-WO-4406`), with or without `hwdef.h`, before anything is judged. A parameter defaults file overrides `var_info` at boot (`AP_Param.cpp:2612-2622`), and a `hwdef.dat` reaches one two ways (`AET-WO-4405` D4): a `defaults.parm` beside it (or a `ROMFS` line) puts it in ROMFS, and `env DEFAULT_PARAMETERS` with `AP_PARAM_MAX_EMBEDDED_PARAM` has waf write it into `AP_Param::param_defaults_data`. The refusal names every signature found: `AP_Param::load_defaults_file` or `AP_Param::load_embedded_param_defaults` linked, `param_defaults_data`'s length not 0, `AP_ROMFS::files` listing `defaults.parm`; and, for Lua, which can set parameters at boot (ruled on the work order's PR), ROMFS listing any `scripts/*.lua` (`boards.py:669-672`, `lua_scripts.cpp:548-550`) or `AP_Scripting::var_info`'s `ENABLE` compiling to a default other than 0, which runs a script from the SD card too (`spike/4406`: none of these on any committed or CI-built board). `reload_defaults_file` is in every build and is not one. **One file is admitted** (`AET-WO-4412`, ruled 2026-11-26T17:35Z): `check pinout` hands `elf::read_admitting` the `defaults.parm` that `generate hwdef` writes for the topology (a DShot board's `MOT_PWM_TYPE`). The ROMFS copy is admitted only if it inflates (raw deflate, `AP_ROMFS.cpp`) to exactly that file, byte for byte, and `load_defaults_file` is admitted with it, since on ChibiOS it loads only that file (`AP_HAL_ChibiOS/Util.h:43-45`, `AP_Param.cpp:1599-1632`). Any other content is refused, naming its first differing line, and every other signature still refuses. A file another engine version wrote (the version on its first line) is refused naming both versions, with whether anything else differs once they agree: regenerate the hwdef with this engine and rebuild (`AET-WO-1802`). To name them, a file whose stated size is within 32 bytes of the topology's is inflated, and no larger one. A ROMFS that is absent lists nothing; one that cannot be read is refused. Values saved in parameter storage, and other boot-time paths, are not seen (`AET-WO-4406` FEEDBACK). A port whose RC consumer is compiled out is not seen either (`AET-WO-4405` D5).
- **`USART_CR2` at run time is read at source, not measured:** RXINV, TXINV and SWAP reach it through `set_options` (`UARTDriver.cpp:1601-1635`).
- **No SPI speed floor:** the generator refuses a speed below ArduPilot's slowest divider (D3). `HAL_I2Cn_CONFIG`'s two DMA branches must agree on the bus number, which is all the judge reads of them. With no `hwdef.h`, the verdict's `identity` says it was not judged.

**What it cannot see:**
- a symbol table that lies: a `st_value` pointing at a decoy copy of good bytes passes on the ELF alone. For `gpio_default_config` only the `hwdef.h` cross-check catches it, and CI always passes `hwdef.h`; for `var_info` nothing does;
- which MCU the firmware was built for: an H743 or H757 image decodes like an H753;
- a pin reconfigured at run time, including ArduPilot's `ALT(n)` sets (`HAL_PIN_ALT_CONFIG`);
- a declared allow-list pin carrying a second copy of a topology signal;
- a pin's drive outside the topology: an allow-listed pin is judged on mode and AF only, and so are PA13 and PA14;
- an `ODR` bit `low` as solved: every `gpio` entry the check admits is a chip select at `high` (`GpioNotJudged` refuses the rest), so the level rule is exercised on `high` alone;
- the battery divider on the board: the judge holds `hwdef.h` to the declared resistances, not the copper;
- whether `hwdef.h`'s `OTYPER`, `OSPEEDR`, `PUPDR` and `ODR` agree with the ELF: the cross-check covers `MODER`, `AFRL` and `AFRH`;
- a pull resistor on an input the topology does not name;
- whether a driver uses the bus its line names.

**Separation (D7, `4400b` D8).** This crate depends on `aethelgard-ir` alone. `tests/separation.rs` reads Cargo's resolve and fails if the check reaches any other workspace crate, if anything but the CLI links it, or if the `hwdef.dat` generator (`aethelgard-hwdef`) reaches anything but `aethelgard-ir`.

**Fixtures.** `fixtures/` holds the four `hwdef.dat` files of the `AET-WO-4400` spike: step G and the seeds `seed-pin`, `seed-af` and `seed-pkg`. They are this project's own. `step-g.allow.json` declares the USART3 pins step G needs. CI builds the four in the ArduPilot image and judges them (`.github/workflows/ardupilot-image.yml`). They carry no speed or pull tokens, so since `4400b` each fails on ArduPilot's default speeds (`10`) and pull-ups beside its seed's own findings. Since `AET-WO-4410` each holds the SWD lines. `tests/common/topology.json` is `aethelgard solve --file examples/sentinel-01-minimal.json`, regenerated at every solved-schema change (`4300j` R9): the identity tests take it with its console removed, since they declare every port they judge. The boards that pass are the three `aethelgard-hwdef` generates. No ArduPilot binary is committed.
