# aethelgard-ir

The specification schema and the solved topology, as two separate types (`RUL-16`), with validation. Every type refuses unknown fields and accepts only a JSON object. Nothing defaults (`INV-06`).

## `Specification`, version 23

Version 2 (`AET-WO-4100`) added `board`. Version 3 (`AET-WO-4300c`) requires a net constraint on every SPI bus's `sck` and `mosi`, whose capacitance and rise time choose the pin's output speed, and names parts as the version-2 parts table does (`W25Q64JVxIQ`). It refuses content version 2 admitted: an SPI bus's line with no constraint (`MissingSpiConstraint`, `solve`'s then, `validate`'s since `AET-WO-1207`) or on a supply outside the speed table (`SupplyOutsideSpeedTable`), the bare `W25Q64JV` (`AmbiguousPartName`), a table part in another case (`PartNameCase`), and a part's limits: its supply (`SupplyOutsidePart`), its bus (`PartOnWrongBus`), its I2C address (`I2cAddressNotOffered`), its SPI clock (`SpiClockAbovePart`), and a constraint on a MISO or chip-select net (`UnjudgedSpiConstraint`). Version 4 (`AET-WO-4300f`) adds `connectors`. Version 5 (`AET-WO-1308`) requires `net_classes` and `diff_pairs`; an empty array declares none. Version 6 (`AET-WO-4300a`) requires `mcu.hse_hz` (the crystal's frequency, `0` for none, the only value admitted until a crystal part existed, `4400` D1; version 20 replaced it) and `motor_outputs`: each a `label` (its net), a `pin` (`null` for the solver), a `protocol` (`pwm`, `dshot300`, `dshot600`) and `pwm` (`{rate_hz, pulse_min_us, pulse_max_us}` for PWM, `null` for DShot). A device reference shaped `p<port><pin>obs` is reserved (`InvalidIdentifier`). Version 7 (`AET-WO-4300d`) requires `mcu.bor_level` (0 to 3) and `power`, whose value `null` declares no power tree. Version 8 (`AET-WO-4210`) requires `accept_lifecycle`: each `eol` or `nrnd` part the board uses, as `{part, status, statement}` with a non-empty statement; `validate` refuses an unacknowledged one by name (`LifecycleNotAccepted`, `LifecycleStatusMismatch`), and an empty array acknowledges none. It also refuses a device whose bus is outside its part's I/O range (`IoOutsidePart`). Version 9 (`AET-WO-4300b`) requires `serial_ports` (below); an empty array declares none. Version 10 (`AET-WO-4300d3`) adds to `power`: `conditions.dc_bias_derating` (each `{dielectrics, ratio_max_permille, retained_permille, source}`), `input.hot_plug` (each `{reference, part, side}`, `side` `board` or `pack`), and on each regulator `junction` (`{copper_square_centimetres, loss_milliwatts}`, each `{value, source}`; `null` for an LDO) and `controls`, as on each crossing (each `{pin, strap, wire}`, exactly one of `strap` and `wire` `null`; `wire` is `{net, role}`). Under a declared `power` it judges content version 9 admitted: the supply's floor against the brown-out's rising threshold (`SupplyFloorBelowBorRising`), each ceramic capacitor at its working voltage (`EffectiveCapacitanceBelowMinimum`, `DcBiasBeyondCurve`), a battery-fed input (`BatteryInputUndamped`), each control pin of a part's row (`ControlPinUnwired`) and a buck's junction (`BuckJunctionOverLimit`). Version 11 (`AET-WO-4300g`, renumbered from 9) requires `mcu.supply_source`: `{connector, pin}`, the declared connector pin the supply enters by, or `null` when a regulator of `power` outputs the supply. A file needs a connector carrying `SWDIO`, `SWCLK`, `GND` and the supply (`NoProgrammingPath`) and a source for the supply (`SupplyHasNoSource`), and `SWDIO`, `SWCLK` and `NRST` are the engine's nets (`DuplicateNetName`, `NetShadowsDomain`). Version 12 (`AET-WO-4230`, renumbered from 10, then from 11, by the same rule) requires `peer_deviation_ppm` and `peer_deviation_source` on every serial port, and `acknowledge_unwitnessed` (below; an empty array acknowledges none). It tightens `SerialBaudTolerance` (HSI's deviation and the peer's are added) and `PullUpBelowSinkFloor` (the sink current at the domain's maximum), and `solve` refuses an SPI bus below its kernel's range or with no kernel clock (`SpiSpeedBelowKernelRange`, `SpiKernelClockUnavailable`). Version 13 (`AET-WO-4220b`) requires `pin_capacitance` on every I2C and SPI device (below). Version 14 (`AET-WO-1312`, renumbered from 13 by the same rule) adds `test_points` (each `{net, reference}`: a pad on a net a connector pin may name, step 15a) and `mcu.reset_switch` (a push-button from `NRST` to `GND`); both may be absent, as that work order states, which declares no test point and no switch, and neither is written back when empty or `false`. `BOOT0` joins the engine's debug nets (`DuplicateNetName`, `NetShadowsDomain`). Version 15 (`AET-WO-1313`) adds `board.max_outline_um` (`{width, height}`, micrometres as the rest of `board`, each 1 to 1 000 000; `OutlineBound` otherwise), optional by the precedent ruled on PR #191 (Q1): absent is today's placer, it is not written back, and an explicit `null` is refused as `test_points` and `mcu.reset_switch` refuse it. A declared bound that cannot hold the parts at all (one keep-out box, or their sum, inside the bound less the placer's 2 mm margin) is refused by name, `OutlineTooSmall`, before any placement: by `emit` always, and by the CLI's `validate` when the specification solves; one that does not solve is left to `solve` and `emit` to refuse by their own names (ruled F3). A bound the placer does not reach is `emit`'s `OutlineUnreachable`. Version 16 (`AET-WO-4400d`, ruled on PR #207) adds `board.forward`, the edge the vehicle's nose points to as the board file draws it seen from F.Cu (`top`, the edge at the least y, `right`, `bottom` or `left`), and an SPI device's `orientation`, `{x, z}`: where the chip's own +X and +Z point in the board frame, each `forward`, `aft`, `right`, `left`, `up` or `down` (Y is Z × X). **The board frame is the engine's constant (ruled D3):** x points to `board.forward`, y to its right, and z down, through F.Cu to B.Cu, so the board is mounted F.Cu up; a board mounted any other way is ArduPilot's `AHRS_ORIENTATION`, a parameter outside this engine. Both keys are optional by the same precedent and an explicit `null` is refused. An undeclared IMU is placed as before and refused by `generate hwdef` (`ImuOrientationUndeclared`); a declared one whose footprint cannot be turned to it is the placer's `ImuRotationUnplaceable`. Version 17 (`AET-WO-4400g` §1.2, Q1 ruled on PR #229) adds `mcu.foreign_clock`, the kernel clocks a foreign firmware programs, each `{value, source}` in Hz: `apb1_timers_hz` and `apb2_timers_hz` (RM0433 Rev 9 Table 57's `rcc_timx_ker_ck` and `rcc_timy_ker_ck`) and `usarts`, one entry per USART the platform carries, keyed `USART1`, `USART2`, `USART3`, `UART4`, `UART5`, `USART6`, `UART7` and `UART8`. It is optional by the same precedent and an explicit `null` is refused. `simulate --foreign` refuses a specification without it by name, and a run of the generated firmware refuses one with it (`INV-06`); the twin builds every timer and USART at it, and the foreign judge checks the timers against the RCC registers at the run's end. Version 18 (`AET-WO-2304b` §1.2) requires `power.battery.series_milliohms`, the healthy battery path's series resistance (the pack, its leads, the input connector and the `VBAT` copper) as `{value, source}` in mΩ with a non-zero value, or `null` (undeclared), and `esc` on every motor output: `{"none": {"source"}}` (no ESC on the board's path, and why) or `null` (undeclared). Both keys are required, because their absence would relax the battery-sag check; `validate` judges nothing else with either, and a battery sag halts by name on a `null` (`SeriesResistanceUndeclared`, `EscUndeclared`). Version 19 (`AET-WO-4240v`, D-1 and D-4 ruled at writing; renumbered from 18 by the same rule, merging after `2304b`) requires `mcu.core_supply`, `{mode, source}`: `mode` `ldo` or `bypass`, the two legal rows of RM0433 Rev 9 Table 34 (p. 261), and `source` where the mode was read (1 to 200 bytes of printable ASCII, cited with a document number and its revision). `bypass` validates and solves; `generate`, `emit` and `simulate` refuse it (`CoreSupplyNotGenerated`). A declared `mcu.foreign_clock` also requires `vos`, `{value, source}`, `value` `scale0` to `scale3`: the voltage scale the foreign firmware runs its core at. Version 20 (`AET-WO-4240a`, `4240` D4 ruled on #240) replaces `mcu.hse_hz` with `mcu.hse`, a required key, for which `hse_hz: 0` alone still stands (below): `null` (no HSE; the HSI as before), `{"crystal": {part, load_caps_femtofarads, pull_ppm, temperature_celsius, life_hours}}` (`part` a `crystal` row of the power parts table; `load_caps_femtofarads` `[CL1, CL2]` declared by value, the capacitors' part being `4240c`'s; `pull_ppm` the declared pulling, at least 1, since no held datasheet states pullability; `temperature_celsius` `{min, max}`, inside the row's range; `life_hours`, at most the span the row's aging is stated for), or `{"external": {hz_num, hz_den, ppm, source}}` (an exact frequency, 4 to 50 MHz, and its cited deviation). `hse_hz: 0`, the only value version 19 admitted, reads as `null`, so the committed specifications moved by their version line alone; any other `hse_hz`, or `hse_hz` beside `hse`, is refused at parsing. An HSE validates and solves. Version 21 (`AET-WO-1210`, renumbered from 20 by the same rule, merging after `4240a`) adds `board.drawn`, optional by the same precedent: absent, or `true`, is a board drawn from the specification, which `validate` holds to step 23 and the CLI's drawable-parts rule; `false` declares a twin stand-in no board is drawn from, which neither rule holds, and which `emit`, `extract` and `verify` (in every mode) refuse by name (`DrawsNoBoard`), and for which `generate` writes its board crate and platform and no `kicad/`. `true` is not written back, and an explicit `null` is refused. Because an absent key is a drawn board, a version-20 file is held to step 23 (`UnboundedPinCapacitance`) and the CLI's drawable-parts rule (`PartNotDrawable`). Version 22 (`AET-WO-1211a`, merging after `1210`) adds `pin_devices` (below), which may be absent, declaring none, and requires `idle` and `resolution_ps` in every PWM output's `pwm` (below); the committed specifications moved by their version line and, on their 8 PWM outputs, by `{"pulse_us": pulse_min_us}` and 1,000,000, which restate the old words. Version 23 (`AET-WO-4240b` §1.1, Q1 ruled 2026-11-20; renumbered from 22 by the same rule, merging after `1211a`) requires `startup_max_us` and `startup_source` on a declared `crystal` and on a declared `external`: the generated bring-up's `HSERDY` bound in µs, which DS12117 states only as a typical, and where it comes from (1 to 200 bytes of printable ASCII); a 0 is `HseDeclarationZero`. A topology whose clock is not the one its own HSE declaration solves to is refused (`HseCopyNotSolved`, `4240a` F3). On an HSE board the SPI kernels run at the HSE's budget, so `solve` may refuse a version-22 bus (`SpiSpeedBelowKernelRange`); and `AET-WO-1214` added at this version a MAVLink or GPS baud ArduPilot reads as kbaud (`SerialBaudReadAsKbaud`). A file of another version is refused by name (`UnsupportedSchemaVersion`), an older one with every version change it lacks (`versions::CHANGES`, `AET-WO-1215`): for each version after its own, in order, the work order and the paths it requires, adds as optional, replaces or removes, and the refusals it added on content the version before admitted, in `message` and in the CLI's `error.entries`. A path is `.`-separated keys with `[]` for each element of an array, required wherever its parent is present; a row names paths, never a value. Version 0, which never existed, and a newer version are refused as before, with no row; nothing supplies a board, a declaration, a connector list, a class list or a motor output list for it. Every key is required, but `test_points` and `mcu.reset_switch` (version 14), `pin_devices` (version 22), `board.max_outline_um` (version 15), `board.forward` and an SPI device's `orientation` (version 16), and `mcu.foreign_clock` (version 17), and `board.drawn` (version 21), and eight that `AET-WO-1303b` and `AET-WO-1314` added at version 16 with no bump: `board.stackup`'s `outer_copper_min_um`, `plane_copper_min_um`, `plating_min_um`, `copper_temperature_max_c` and `fab_tolerances`, a dielectric's `max_thickness_um`, `power.drop_limits` and a load's `reference`. `peripheral`, `pin`, `impedance_ohms` and a signal layer's `net` may be `null`, but the key may not be missing. Arrays keep the order they were written in; nothing is sorted.

**`connectors`** (`AET-WO-4300f`, ruled on PR #134). Where a net leaves the board. Each connector names its `reference`, its `part` (a row of `connectors::PARTS`: JST GH `SM04B-GHS-TB`, `SM06B-GHS-TB`, `SM10B-GHS-TB` and JST SH `SM06B-SRSS-TB`, each cited by its catalogue's sha256), its `standard` (a row of `ds009::PORTS` by name, or `null` for an explicit map), and every signal pin, `1` to N, once:

```json
{ "reference": "J1", "part": "SM04B-GHS-TB", "standard": "DS-009 I2C", "pins": [
  { "pin": "1", "net": "VDD_5V", "offboard": null },
  { "pin": "2", "net": "I2C_SCL", "offboard": { "capacitance_femtofarads": 30000, "source": "condition: …" } },
  { "pin": "3", "net": "I2C_SDA", "offboard": { "capacitance_femtofarads": 30000, "source": "condition: …" } },
  { "pin": "4", "net": "GND", "offboard": null } ] }
```

- A pin's `net` is a declared signal net, a voltage domain or `GND`, or `null` for a pin left unconnected. A connector cannot invent a net.
- A pin on an I2C or SPI line (a bus line or a chip select) declares its `offboard` capacitance, with its source: the mated contact, the cable and the remote pins, at most. Extraction adds it to the board's copper on every line it bounds (`RUL-19` (1)). Any other pin's `offboard` is `null`.
- The mounting pads are not declared. The emitter puts them on `GND`.

Each I2C and SPI device carries `pin_capacitance` (version 13, `AET-WO-4220b`): `null`, or a declaration for a part whose datasheet prints its pin capacitance as a typical only, or not at all (the `AET-WO-4220` D10 ruling):

```json
"pin_capacitance": { "declared": { "femtofarads": 10000,
  "condition": "each pin at most TDK's typical C_I of < 10 pF, which no maximum bounds",
  "source": "TDK DS-000347 v1.9 Table 4, p. 14: C_I < 10 pF in the TYP column" } }
```

- `extract` counts the declared value for each of the device's pins on every line it bounds: an SPI device's on SCK and MOSI, an I2C device's on SDA and SCL in place of UM10204's `Ci`, never below it (`PinCapacitanceBelowI2cCi`). The verdict lists each such pin as declared, with its condition and source, and states that the bound holds only under it; a declared value is never named a maximum (`RUL-19`).
- A part whose parts-table row cites a maximum is counted at that maximum (`AET-WO-4300c` D3), and a declaration for it is refused. A declaration below the typical the part's row cites is refused on any bus (`PinCapacitanceBelowTypical`): the ICM-42688-P's and ICM-20948's C_I "< 10" pF TYP, held as 10 pF, and the TMP117's 4 pF. An SPI device whose part cites none and declares none is refused by `extract` (`UnboundedPinCapacitance`); an I2C device without one is counted at UM10204's `Ci`, as before.
- A `standard` holds the connector to DS-009's pinout for that port. DS-009 rev 0.1.0 is cited by its only commit `48dd6d1d` and its sha256, never vendored, and its mark is used nowhere (ruled D2, provisionally). The ports are `DS-009 TELEM`, `DS-009 GPS`, `DS-009 GPS FULL`, `DS-009 I2C` and `DS-009 DEBUG MINI`.
  - A supply pin must be a domain of the voltage DS-009 states: +5 V for the external ports (ruled D5), and the microcontroller's supply for the debug port.
  - A `TX (OUT)` pin takes a serial port's `tx` net and an `RX (IN)` pin its `rx` net, both of one port: DS-009's tables are the autopilot's side, and the peripheral crosses the lines (Telemetry Port). So `DS-009 TELEM`, `DS-009 GPS` (whose I2C pins take one bus's lines) and `DS-009 I2C` validate.
  - A role this schema cannot yet declare (a safety switch, a LED, a buzzer, SWD) takes no net, so `DS-009 GPS FULL` and `DS-009 DEBUG MINI` are refused until the work order that declares it. CTS and RTS may be `null`, as DS-009 lets them float.
  - Everything that judges a standard is `ds009.rs`. Explicit maps only (ruling (c)) would be that module, the field and its four refusals deleted.

A connector is in the `Specification` only. Nothing solves it, so the twin, which reads only the `SolvedTopology`, never sees one.

**`power`** (`AET-WO-4300d`, ruled on PR #137). The battery, the power-in connector (`connectors::POWER_IN`), each regulator with its feedback divider and external parts, declared loads, the battery sense and the bus crossings. Each part is a row of the power parts table, `data/power-parts.json`, read by `power_parts::parse_power_parts`; every value there is cited to a page of a document held by sha256. The shape and the order of the checks are `power.rs`'s module documentation. Every refusal is `IrValidationError::Power`, whose `kind()` names the check. A specification that declares a tree is refused by the emitter (`PowerNotEmitted`) until the emission work order lands.
**`pin_devices`** (`AET-WO-1211a`, `1211` Q1–Q10 as ruled). Each a device with no bus: a driver IC bound to a busless row of the parts table (`data/parts.json` `pin_parts`, `pin_parts.rs`), whose pins, kinds, pulls, levels, timing and per-mode truth tables are row data cited to the maker's held document; a value no held document states is not entered. The list may be absent, which declares none, and is not written back when empty. Each entry: `reference`; `part`; `supplies`, each supply pin to a domain; `straps`, each strap pin (and current-sense return) to `{"tie": "GND" | <domain>}` or `{"resistor": {reference, part, ohms}}` to ground; `signals`, each `{device_pin, net, pin, direction, …}`, an `output` with `initial` (`low` or `high`, written before `MODER`) and an `input` with `active` and `pull` (`{"mcu": "up" | "down" | "none", source}` or `{"resistor": {reference, part, ohms, to}, source}`); `analog`, each `{device_pin, net, pin, channel, transfer, full_scale, adc_resolution_bits}` with `channel` `null` or `{peripheral, input}`, `transfer` `{"current_output": {resistor, reservoir}}` and `full_scale` `{"milliamps": n}`; `bridges`, each `{id, mode, inputs, outputs}`, `mode` a mode of the row (`ph_en`, `in_in`), `inputs` each input pin to a PWM motor output's label or this device's output signal's net (`1211` Q7), `outputs` each output pin to a net; `part_limits` (`{"input_pwm_max_hz": null | {value, source}}`, a limit the row does not state, declared with its citation); and `temperature_celsius`, the range its resistors are judged over, `null` when `power` declares the board's. A bridge judges its idle only: each input idles at a `level`, and the idle tuple and the reset tuple are non-driving rows of the truth table in its strapped mode (`1211` Q3). Every other crate refuses a pin device by name until its work order lands.

**`motor_outputs[].pwm.idle` and `.resolution_ps`** (`AET-WO-1211a`, `1211` Q4, Q5). Required on every PWM output, with no default. `idle` is `{"level": "low"}`, `{"level": "high"}` or `{"pulse_us": n}` with `n` above 0: what the output carries from `CEN` until its first command, separate from the commanded range. `resolution_ps` is the timer tick in picoseconds, exact at the kernel clock (`ResolutionNotDerivable`), with every pulse and the idle a whole number of ticks (`PulseNotOnTick`, `IdleNotOnTick`) and the idle inside the period (`IdleExceedsPeriod`). `{"pulse_us": pulse_min_us}` with 1,000,000 restates what the firmware wrote before, so no solved word of a committed specification moved. `generate` refuses an idle `high` (`IdleHighNotGenerated`) until `AET-WO-1211b`, because the generator's motor pull is a pull-down and `silicon::idle_pupd` follows the idle.

**`serial_ports`** (`AET-WO-4300b`). Each a USART or UART with a TX and an RX line, the solver choosing what is `null`:

```json
{ "label": "CONSOLE", "peripheral": null,
  "tx": { "net": "CONSOLE_TX", "pin": null }, "rx": { "net": "CONSOLE_RX", "pin": null },
  "role": "console",
  "framing": { "baud": 115200, "data_bits": 8, "parity": "none", "stop_bits": 1, "inverted": false },
  "negotiated": null, "peer_deviation_ppm": 5000, "peer_deviation_source": "declared" }
```

- `peripheral` is `null` or one of `serial::PERIPHERALS` exactly as written: `USART1`, `USART2`, `USART3`, `UART4`, `UART5`, `USART6`, `UART7`, `UART8`. `LPUART1`, in any case, is refused by its own name: its enable bit is in `RCC_APB4ENR`, which the generated firmware does not write, and its baud generator is not a USART's.
- `role` is `console`, `mavlink`, `gps`, `rc_crsf` or `rc_sbus`. The framing is declared, never inferred from it. `rc_crsf` must be 8N1, not inverted, at 416 666 baud or above (TBS's CRSF specification, dual-wire, held by sha256, `serial::CRSF_SOURCE`), and at a baud of the cited set `serial::CRSF_BAUDS` (ruled on PR #152): 416 666 (TBS) or 420 000 (the default of ExpressLRS receivers and Betaflight, `serial::CRSF_ELRS_SOURCE`). Any other baud is refused (432 000 among them) unless the port declares it negotiated. SBUS has no vendor-published source, so nothing is refused on its behalf.
- `negotiated` is required: `null`, or `{ "source": "…" }` on an `rc_crsf` port whose baud is a rate the two ends negotiate (CRSF frame 0x70, "CRSF Protocol Speed Proposal"), with a source for the rate held to the stackup sources' rule (1 to 200 bytes of printable ASCII, no space at either end). It is refused on any other role (`SerialNegotiatedOffCrsf`), with any other source, empty included (`InvalidSource`, naming `serial_ports[i].negotiated.source`, never the value), and at a baud of `serial::CRSF_BAUDS` (`SerialNegotiatedAtCitedBaud`). `solve` copies it to the solved port.
- `peer_deviation_ppm` is required (`u32`): the deviation of the remote transmitter's baud clock from the declared baud, a declaration. The check for silicon (`serial::check_silicon`) sums the quantization error, HSI's worst-case deviation in the direction that adds (DS12117 Rev 10 Table 36 rev Y / Table 133 rev V: +14,984 / −25,894 ppm over voltage and junction temperature −40 °C to TJmax) and this, and requires the sum strictly below RM0433 Rev 9's Table 406/407 tolerance; `solve` refuses a port that fails it (`SerialBaudTolerance`, naming each term), and the finding (`SiliconFraming`) reports them. An `rc_crsf` port declares at least `serial::crsf_cited_spread_ppm()`, the spread of the two bauds `CRSF_BAUDS` cites (8,002 ppm); a lower figure is refused (`PeerDeviationBelowCited`). The fixtures declare 5,000 ppm for a console, MAVLink, GPS and SBUS peer, a declaration with no datasheet behind it.
- `peer_deviation_source` is required: `cited` or `declared` (`AET-WO-4230` R2, the architect's ruling on Q-A). `cited` stands only where the repository holds the citation: an `rc_crsf` port whose `peer_deviation_ppm` is exactly the cited spread; any other `cited` is refused (`PeerDeviationNotCited`). Every other figure is `declared`, and `verify`'s verdict names it.
- `framing`: `baud` above 0; `data_bits` 7 to 9, and with a parity bit a word of 7 to 9 bits (`USART_CR1.M`); `parity` `none`, `even` or `odd`; `stop_bits` 1 or 2; `inverted` sets both `TXINV` and `RXINV`.
- The TX and RX nets are signal nets like any other: each ends at a connector pin, which declares no `offboard` (D8 is I2C and SPI only), and takes no net constraint (no check reads one).

**`acknowledge_unwitnessed`** (`AET-WO-4230` R1, the architect's ruling on PR #161, comment 6911424608). Each register's fields that the witness ledger classes `ignored-by-model`, `same-source` or `silicon-only`, acknowledged per register and class:

```json
{ "register": "SPI1.CFG2", "class": "ignored-by-model", "fields": ["CPHA", "CPOL"],
  "reason": "Renode stores neither (a tag): the bench reads them back" }
```

`register` is the ledger's name for it (a peripheral and a register joined by one `.`, each an upper-case letter then upper-case letters, digits or `_`); `class` one of the three; `fields` the exact, non-empty list without repeats; `reason` held to the stackup sources' rule (1 to 200 bytes of printable ASCII, no space at either end). At most one entry per register and class. `validate` judges only this shape; the CLI's `validate` refuses an entry naming a field the ledger lacks for that register and class (`UnwitnessedAcknowledgementStale`), and `verify --fab-out` a ledger row of those classes no entry names (`UnwitnessedFieldNotAcknowledged`). `Specification::unwitnessed_acknowledgement(register, class)` reads an entry, typed by `UnwitnessedClass`.

**`net_classes`, `diff_pairs`** (`AET-WO-1308` §1.1, §1.3; FEEDBACK F2 to F4). Micrometres. A class gives its nets a track width and, optionally, a via (`via` is a required key, `null` for the rules' via); a pair gives two nets, `[positive, negative]`, a gap and a width. Every class's clearance is `board.rules.clearance_um`, so none is declared (F2).

**`board`** (`AET-WO-4100`, ruled on PR #120). Micrometres and thousandths. A stackup value is a bound with its source, never a nominal: a minimum dielectric is its source's value less a published tolerance, rounded down; a maximum is rounded up. The engine builds 2 copper layers (both signal) or 4 (`F.Cu`, `In1.Cu`, `In2.Cu`, `B.Cu`: outer layers signal, both inner layers planes, exactly one `GND` and the other a declared domain), through vias only. The rules are honoured: clearance and track width at or above the engine floor (0.2 mm), and the one via the engine builds (0.3 mm drill, 0.6 mm). See `crates/aethelgard-cli/tests/fixtures/four-layer.json` for a 4-layer board on OSH Park's published stackup.

## `SolvedTopology`

What the solver derives (`RUL-16`):
- `schema_version` (16; an older file is refused as `UnsupportedSchemaVersion`, whether or not it has the keys a later version added);
- `specification_sha256` (64 lowercase hex digits);
- `assignments`, a `BTreeMap` keyed by net, holding every bus signal on an alternate function, each value a `PinAssignment` (`pin`, `peripheral`, `signal`, `alternate_function`);
- `gpio` (version 14, `AET-WO-1211a`), a `BTreeMap` keyed by net, holding every net on a plain GPIO: `{pin, direction, initial}` for an output (`initial` `low` or `high`, the level written before `MODER`) and `{pin, direction, pull}` for an input (`pull` the MCU's own, `up`, `down` or `none`). Each SPI device's `cs` is an output at `high`; a pin device's signals are the rest. With no pin device, `validate` refuses any entry but a `high` output (`PinDeviceUndeclared`), so a reader that refuses a pin device reads every entry as a chip select;
- `adc_inputs` (version 14, `AET-WO-1211a`), a `BTreeMap` keyed by net: each ADC input's `pin`, the `peripheral` the solver chose (ADC1, then ADC3; ADC2 only on a pin the specification pins, `1211` Q8) and its `channel` (`INPm`). It holds a pin device's analog inputs and each `power.sense` net, which keeps its place in `power`;
- `i2c_ceilings`, a `BTreeMap` from I2C bus id to that bus's maximum speed in Hz (100,000, 400,000 or 1,000,000). It is empty when there are no I2C buses;
- `i2c_speeds`, a `BTreeMap` from I2C bus id to the speed the specification declared for it (`speed_hz`), under the same keys as `i2c_ceilings` (`AET-WO-1205`). Required, never defaulted;
- `analog_lines`, `i2c_devices` and `spi_devices` (`AET-WO-2200`, `2203`, `4200`); an SPI device carries its declared `orientation` (version 10, `AET-WO-4400d`), absent when none is declared, so that `generate hwdef` and `check pinout` read it without the specification (`RUL-16`);
- `spi_lines` (version 4, `AET-WO-4300c`), a `BTreeMap` keyed by net holding each SPI bus's SCK and MOSI: its `bus`, `signal`, `rate_hz` (the bus's `speed_hz`), the declared `capacitance_femtofarads` and `rise_time_ns`, and `vdd_min_millivolts`, the MCU supply's nominal less its tolerance, rounded down. The generator chooses each pin's output speed from it alone (`RUL-16`).
- `clock` (version 5, `AET-WO-4300a` §1.2), the clock the generated firmware runs on, as a solved fact: the reset clock, HSI at 64 MHz with every prescaler 1 and `TIMPRE` 0 (RM0433 Rev 9 §8.7.2, §8.7.7–§8.7.10), so every timer's kernel clock is 64 MHz (Table 57), and `vos` (version 12, `AET-WO-4240v`), the core's voltage scale, `scale2`: the bring-up raises it from the reset `scale3` before any peripheral. `validate` first judges every bus clock and every enabled kernel against DS12117 Rev 10 Table 111 and RM0433 Rev 9 Table 60 at `vos`, each at its fastest (HSI +14,984 ppm, rounded up), and `FLASH_ACR` at reset against Table 17 (`VosLimitExceeded`, the clock; `FlashWaitStatesBelowTable`), then refuses any tree but the reset one at `scale2` (`UnsupportedClock`). No older file is given a clock: one without `clock` is refused at parsing. **The HSE tree** (version 13, `AET-WO-4240a`): with `mcu.hse` declared, `clock` is H1 (`4240` D1): `source` `PLL1`, `sysclk_hz` 64 MHz, `hse_hz` the source's whole Hz, every prescaler 1, and `hse`, `{declared, fast_ppm, slow_ppm, pllckselr, pllcfgr, pll1divr}`: the declaration copied (`RUL-16`), its budget (`clock::declared_budget`: a crystal's tolerance + stability + aging from its row, plus the declared pull, per side; an external clock's ppm alone), and the solver's words (the least `DIVM1` whose reference is a whole number of Hz strictly inside a `PLL1RGE` range, the medium VCO under 2 MHz, the least even `DIVP1` reaching 64 MHz, `DIVQ1` = `DIVP1`). `hse` is absent, not `null`, when none is declared, so such a topology differs only by its version. Every timing check (serial, DShot and PWM, I2C `TIMINGR`, the VOS rule) takes the clock's budget (`SolvedClock::budget`); SPI stays on `hsi_ker_ck` and the HSI's, the VOS rule included, until `4240b`. `clock::judge_tree` shares no word-choice code with the solver (`solve::h1_words`); the budget is one function both call (`4240` D3, ruled: it replaced the `belt-and-suspenders` copies, and hand-written figures in the tests stand in for them). It re-derives the budget, refuses any bit of the three words outside the fields it reads and a `DIVR1` of 0 (`PllWordsUndecodable`), holds `pll1_q_ck` to SPI1–3's kernel limit at Scale 2 (`VosLimitExceeded`), and refuses by name a reference not strictly inside its range (`PllReferenceNotInsideRange`), a select whose input range excludes it (`PllVcoSelectMismatch`), a VCO outside its select's range (`PllVcoOutOfRange`; wide 192–836 MHz, the stricter silicon revision), `DIVP1EN` 0 (`PllOutputDisabled`), a source other than HSE or a fractional PLL (`PllWordsUndecodable`), and any field the words, decoded by `SolvedClock::from_rcc`, do not give exactly (`HseClockMismatch`). AN2867's oscillation margin is reported, uncited, and judges nothing;
- `core_supply` (version 12, `AET-WO-4240v`), `mcu.core_supply.mode`, copied so the generator writes `PWR_CR3` without reading the specification (`RUL-16`); never defaulted;
- `motor_outputs` (version 5), a `BTreeMap` keyed by label (its net, a key of `assignments` on a `TIMn_CHk` signal): `index`, `protocol`, the declared `pwm` (with `idle` and `resolution_ps` from version 14), and the solved `psc`, `arr`, `ccr`: `PSC` from the declared tick, and `CCR` the idle word, 0 for `low`, `ARR + 1` for `high`, else the idle pulse in ticks. `motor::check_silicon` judges those values against the declared timing on `clock`, with silicon's `ARR + 1` (`4300` D3), the tick and the idle exactly;

## `solve`

```rust
pub fn solve(spec: &Specification, table: &PinTable, specification_sha256: &str)
    -> Result<SolvedTopology, SolveError>
```

A pure function: its output depends only on the specification, the table and the hash (`INV-01`). The caller hashes the specification's bytes as it read them (`INV-03`); the solver never sees them. Every collection is a `BTreeMap`, `BTreeSet` or `Vec`, and the search's bound counts nodes, never time, so two solves give the same bytes on any host. `solve_with_limit` takes the node budget as an argument, and exists so tests can reach `BudgetExceeded`.

### Reserved pins

`RESERVED_PINS` are never assigned, and a hand pin on one refuses with `ReservedPin`:
- `PA13` (SWDIO) and `PA14` (SWCLK), so the board can still be programmed and debugged;
- `PC14` and `PC15` (LSE), so the board keeps its 32 kHz crystal.

The HSE pins are reserved as `mcu.hse` declares (`reserved_pins`, `AET-WO-4240a` §1.6): `PH0` (`OSC_IN`) and `PH1` (`OSC_OUT`) beside a crystal, `PH0` alone beside an external clock, whose `OSC_OUT` is free, and neither when it is `null`, so the solver then assigns them like any other pin (`AET-WO-4300a` §1.1). A hand pin on a reserved one is `ReservedPin`. No `null` board's pin moved (measured, `AET-WO-4240a` FEEDBACK).

**Debugging is assumed to be SWD only.** The JTAG-only pins `PA15`, `PB3` and `PB4` stay assignable, as do USB's `PA11` and `PA12`.

### The model

- Every bus takes one **instance** of its kind. An instance is offered when the table carries each of its kind's signals for it on some pin (`SDA` and `SCL`, or `SCK`, `MOSI` and `MISO`); on the STM32H753ZI these are `I2C1` to `I2C4` and `SPI1` to `SPI6`. No two buses share an instance.
- Every bus signal takes an unreserved pin whose table entry carries `<instance>_<SIGNAL>`, with that entry's alternate function.
- Every SPI device's `cs` takes any unreserved pin, as a plain GPIO output, recorded in `gpio`.
- No pin is used twice. A hand-set `peripheral` or `pin` is fixed.

### I2C ceilings (`AET-WO-1202`)

Before the pin search, each I2C bus gets a ceiling from the rise time its board declares:
1. `t_max` is the larger `max_rise_time_nanoseconds` of the `net_constraints` entries for the bus's `sda.net` and `scl.net`, the worse line (`AET-WO-1206` §1.7).
2. The ceiling is the fastest mode whose maximum rise time (NXP UM10204) is at least `t_max`:
   - 1 MHz if `t_max` ≤ 120 ns;
   - 400 kHz if ≤ 300 ns;
   - 100 kHz if ≤ 1000 ns.

Pull-up resistance and capacitance are not read; that physics is the twin's.

### The search order

The order is fixed, and it decides which answer is found:
1. Buses go in document order, I2C before SPI.
2. Within a bus, instances go in ascending name order, then its signals in the order `sda, scl` or `sck, mosi, miso`. Each signal tries pins in the table's key order.
3. Once every bus is placed, the motor outputs, in document order (`AET-WO-4300a`). Each tries the table's timer channels (`TIMn_CH1` to `TIMn_CH4` of a timer with channels; never a complementary `CHkN`) in the order timer number, channel, pin key, taking a free pin, a channel not taken, and a timer that is free or already carries the output's key: its protocol and, for PWM, its rate (outputs of one timer share its counter). A failure backtracks into the buses, so the search stays complete.
4. Once every motor output is, the serial ports, in document order (`AET-WO-4300b`). Each tries its declared peripheral, or each of `serial::PERIPHERALS` the table offers in that order, not taken by another port; on it, a TX pin then an RX pin, each in key order, its hand pin or a free one, never one pin for both. A failure backtracks into the motor outputs and the buses, so the search stays complete; on a specification without serial ports this step places nothing and spends no node, so no committed solve moved.
5. Every `cs` goes last, in `spi_devices` document order. Each takes the first free, unreserved pin in key order. This greedy pass loses no solution, because the buses, the motor outputs and the serial ports use a fixed number of pins.

Each serial port's `BRR` is `serial::solve_brr` on its peripheral's kernel clock: the nearest whole `USARTDIV`, half up, oversampling by 16, from 16 to `0xFFFF`. Its error is then at most `1 / (2 BRR)`: 3.125 % at `BRR` 16 (Table 406, whose tightest row is 3.41 %), 2.94 % from 17 on (Table 407, tightest 3.03 %). The quantization alone therefore never fails the tolerance (`serial::within`), but the check for silicon judges the worst case (`AET-WO-4230` §1.8): the quantization error plus HSI's deviation (up to 25,894 ppm) plus the port's `peer_deviation_ppm`, strictly below the row, so `SerialBaudTolerance` refuses a declaration whose sum reaches it (the small `BRR`s, whose rounding error is largest, and any large declared peer deviation).

Each motor output's `PSC`, `ARR` and `CCR` (`motor::solve_timing`) depend on its protocol and the timer clock alone, which is 64 MHz on every timer of the reset clock: PWM takes a 1 µs tick (`PSC` 63), `ARR` the nearest whole tick count to `1 s / rate` less 1 (silicon counts `ARR + 1`), and `CCR` the idle pulse, `pulse_min_us`; DShot takes `PSC` 0, the bit the nearest whole count of 64 MHz ticks to `1 / bit rate` (107 at 600 kbit/s), and `CCR` the nearest whole count to 3/8 of it (a `0` bit, ArduPilot `Copter-4.7.2` `AP_HAL/RCOutput.h` L406–408).

Names sort in **byte order**: `PA0, PA1, PA10, …, PA15, PA2`. So `SPI2_SCK` takes `PA12` before `PA9`. Instance names sort correctly only while they stay single-digit.

The search backtracks. Before it starts, and again after each bus is placed, every bus still to place must pass three checks:
- it still has an instance whose every signal has a free pin (forward checking);
- the remaining buses can take distinct instances (instance matching);
- their signals can take distinct free pins, each drawn from the union of its candidates over the bus's still-placeable instances (pin matching).

Each check answers only yes or no, so none can change which answer is found. Together they turn the infeasible specifications the `AET-WO-1200` spike found exponential into refusals that take well under a millisecond. Neither matching check alone covers both shapes.

They are not complete. When two instances need the same single pin for one signal, both checks pass, and an infeasible specification can still spend the whole budget (`AET-WO-1200` FEEDBACK A1).

The search tries at most `NODE_LIMIT` (10,000,000) values across the whole solve, including the minimal-set searches.

### Refusals (`SolveError`), in check order

Each check runs in document order: bus signals, then every `cs`. `kind()` is the variant name, except for `InvalidSpecification`, which reports the validation refusal's own kind. `id()` is the single pin, peripheral, bus or part refused, or `None`. `claimants()` lists who is in conflict:
- for pin refusals, the signal's JSON path, such as `i2c_buses[0].sda` or `spi_devices[0].cs`;
- for peripheral refusals, the bus's path, such as `i2c_buses[0]`;
- for the refusals about whole buses, the bus ids.
