# AET-WO-4241 §1.1 — predictions, written before the runs

Written 2026-11-20, before any boot below ran. Committed before the runs, and never edited after them;
the measured values go in `out/` and in the FEEDBACK's table beside these.

**Already measured when this was written (static, not a prediction):** `sh/survey.py` (`out/survey.md`). There are
42 committed specifications with 201 devices; 192 of those devices have a part row with an identity. Of the
192, 78 are unmodelled, all of them **TMP117**, in 28 specifications. The only part that is in the table and has
`renode: null` is TMP117, so **no unmodelled SPI part exists in the table**. The SPI cases below use a spike-only
lever, `AET4241_UNMODEL=<part,...>` in `aethelgard_twin::model::kind` (`sh/instrument.diff`, never in `src`).

## Predictions

1. **The generator.**
   - Old and new firmware differ for every case with a TMP117 (C1–C5, C8): one more read in `main.rs` and a
     different ELF.
   - In C0 they are byte-identical, `main.rs` and ELF.
   - No new firmware carries a configuration or data step for an unmodelled part. With C7's lever, U2 and U4
     get their identity reads only, and U3 its own.
2. **The I2C read to an unmodelled address (C1–C5).**
   - The TMP117 record's status is `STATUS_TXIS` (`0x22020001`) and its value is `0`.
   - Renode logs `[WARNING] i2c1: Unknown slave at address 72.` once, during that step. Renode 1.17.0 models no
     NACK (`AET-WO-2101` S6), so the firmware sees a `TXIS` timeout.
   - That status, value and line are **byte for byte what a modelled device that is absent, or that is
     addressed wrongly, leaves.** In 3305's `wrong-address` run, U2 left `timeout_txis`, value 0, and the line
     for its address.
3. **Time.**
   - One `TXIS` wait is at most `I2C_WAIT_SPINS` = 20,000 reads of `ISR`. I predict it adds 100,000 to 300,000
     instructions (1 to 3 ms at 100 MIPS) before the boot word.
   - C8, with 16 such waits, still writes its boot word inside 100 ms: 16 to 48 ms added. If C8 does not
     generate, that is recorded instead.
4. **Today's judge on the new firmware: modelled verdicts move.** The cause is that the judge places records
   from modelled checks alone.
   - **C1 and C2:** U4's record is now index 2. The judge reads index 1, which is TMP117's (`TXIS` on an SPI
     check, so `Invalid`). U4 fails, and the verdict is `fail`.
   - **The address-72 line.** No modelled I2C status write follows the line, so it falls back to every I2C check
     on the controller: U2's `absent` becomes `[72]`. It is attribution only, so U2 still passes.
   - **C3:** as C1. U2 and U5 both get `absent` `[72]`.
   - **C4:** U5's identity, configuration and data records shift by one record. U5 fails, and so does U4.
   - **C5:** U7's records shift and U7 fails. U7's identity status write is the first to follow the line, so the
     line goes to U7's identity step: its `absent` becomes `[72]`.
5. **The SPI read to an unmodelled chip select (C6).**
   - The controller completes the transfer whatever the device: status `STATUS_OK` and value `0`.
   - The multiplexer logs `Tried to transmit byte 0x… to …` lines inside the step's chip-select window.
   - Today's judge has no SPI check in C6, so nothing claims those lines. The verdict is `pass`, with
     `device_unmodelled` ×2 (U3, U4).
   - An unmodelled SPI read is **byte for byte a modelled SPI device that answered 0**: a mismatch.
6. **Nothing modelled (C7).** Today's judge judges no device record and passes on the boot witnesses alone,
   with `device_unmodelled` ×3 (adversarial pass, third lens).

## The stop this predicts (§1.1, §7)

By prediction 2 (I2C) and prediction 5 (SPI), **the twin's response to an unmodelled read cannot be told apart
from a modelled device that failed.** It is the same status, value and log lines. So §1.1's stop fires as
written, and I stop at it with the bytes and lines. The judge would need three things to name the outcome
without that distinction:

- **The device is classified by the plan, never by the response.** It is "not witnessable" exactly when
  `aethelgard_twin::model::kind(part)` is `None` (the parts table's `renode: null`). That is from the
  specification and the table alone (`INV-07`).
- **Each unmodelled identity record gets its slot, in the firmware's order.** The twin places it with its own
  copy of the layout. Without the slot, every later modelled record shifts (prediction 4).
- **Evidence goes to the unmodelled step that drew it.** An unmodelled I2C step gets a status-word hook (as
  `3305`'s), so its `Unknown slave` line is its own. An unmodelled SPI device's chip-select window is its own,
  so its multiplexer lines go to no modelled step. Without both, a modelled step's `absent` moves (prediction 4).
