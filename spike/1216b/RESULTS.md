## The kit

| file | what it does |
|---|---|
| `walker_dump.rs` | A harness, never committed into a crate: copied into a copy of the tree's `crates/aethelgard-ir/tests/`, it writes the walker's paths (`off_domain::paths`) and `validate`'s verdict for every board `universe.sh` lists, one TSV each (`out/walker-pre/`). |
| `compare_walk.py` | The walker's paths against `1216`'s walk 1 (`spike/1216/out/spec`), board by board, path for path. The key is net, class, rails and the worst millivolts (or "not stated"). Walk 1's `HSE_IN` is the walker's `OSC_IN` (PH0). The pin column is not compared: walk 1 reads `solve`'s pins and the walker the specification's. Walk 1's "no:" rows (a crossing on `mcu.supply`) are not paths, so they are counted and not compared. |
| `f7.py` | F7: walk 2's rows on a drawn pin device, classified by the pin row's kind (an input or open drain is no source). |
| `mutants.py` | One mutant per guard this work order adds, each against its suites, in a copy of the tree. |
| `regen.sh` | The two committed board crates, regenerated by `generate` after the bump. |

## M5. R2 at the fixture's own tolerance (D3)

- CRCW0603_F_K: 1 % + 100 ppm/K × 65 K (D1's −20 to 85 °C from its 20 °C reference) + 2 % drift, 36 500 ppm. 1070 Ω is therefore at most 1109.06 Ω, which the engine's `band` rounds up to **1110 Ω**.
- At the held 3 mA that is **3330 mV**. P3.1 predicted 3328 mV, which is walk 1's float figure (adversarial A5). The engine's whole-ohm ceiling is 2 mV higher, the safe direction.
- At the 3.5 A peak on the typical 1000 µA/A ratio it is 3885 mV, **on the typical ratio only** (no AERR is stated above 2 A).
- A2: the 1.9 A full scale is 1995 µA × 1110 Ω = 2214 mV, against VREF's minimum of 3135 mV.
