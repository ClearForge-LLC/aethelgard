**Not used, and why:**
- **The KiCad symbol library (2):** a library's pin list is not the datasheet the work order names. Using it would answer cells from a third party's drawing of the part.
- **The ChibiOS RP2040 port (1):** another chip.

**The document a ruling would need:** Raspberry Pi Ltd's *RP2350 Datasheet*, the revision current when the ruling is made, held by sha256 in `~/.cache/aet-ds/` like the ST documents. A ruling needs whatever parts of it cover the topics the N and S entries in `LIST.md` turn on:
- pin function selection;
- pad electrical structure;
- clocks and the crystal;
- core voltage;
- brown-out.

These topics come from the list, not from that document. Its contents are not known here.
