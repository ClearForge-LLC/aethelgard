## A. Verified defects

### A1. Nothing binds U32 to U30's pins, D1's "placed at U30" is false on the head board, and the floor rule has no drop term (blocking; §5 Q1) [artifact + reading]

**What is shown and what is not.**
- **Shown** (positions, the emitter's anchors, the rule's arithmetic):
  - the emitter and the rule do not bind U32 to U30;
  - U32 is not at U30.
- **Not shown:** the copper node U32's branch taps. Distance alone is not the discriminator. A microamp branch has no drop of its own, so U32 reads whatever node it taps.
- **What extract says:**
  - `probe/s03-extract.json`'s `VDD_3V3_RF` entry lumps the rail's 505 mA at load U30 (bound 220845 µV), with "the source is where the regulator's current enters the domain (a buck's inductor pad)".
  - It gives no per-sink figure at U32.
- **Hence:** the 2858 mV and 2782 mV below are bounds that hold if U32's tap is upstream of U30's drop. The tap point is untraced.

**What the ruling rested on.** STOP.md D1 says: "It is placed at U30 and senses the rail at U30's pin."

**What was built.** Nothing places U32 near U30.
- `schematic_power.rs:536` (`rail_monitor`) anchors only C95 to U32's VDD pin (`anchor(…, MONITOR_BYPASS_RULE)`, line 552).
- U32 itself is free to the placer.

**The head board.** `~/.cache/aet-4413/inv08/head/examples__sentinel-03/out/sentinel-03.kicad_pcb`:

| Part | Position (mm) | Rail pad |
|---|---|---|
| U30 | (129.04, 36.8) | pad 1 at (120.29, 30.8) |
| U32 | (75.4, 100.15) | pad 3 at (76.54, 100.15) |
| U31 | (80.3, 90.25) | — |
| U13 | (93.54, 43.25) | — |

- U32 is 83.0 mm from U30, 11.0 mm from U31 and 59.7 mm from U13.
- **Partial trace of `VDD_3V3_RF`'s F.Cu/B.Cu tracks** (my own parse; inner-layer segments were not captured; L3.2 did not join any track in my graph, so the parse is incomplete). The tracks form two large components:
  - one spanning x 72–138, y 36–47, around U30;
  - one with U32, C95, U31.14 (45 mm of track from U32.3) and R32.
- Vias on the net run down from about y 48 to y 86.
- **Untraced:** where U32's branch joins, and U32's ground path.

**Why the credit is wrong.**
- `supervisor.rs:513-522` (`judged_against`) passes U30 when `3000 ≤ U32's VIT− min (3003)`.
- U32 compares the voltage at its own pins. U30 draws 505 mA through the rail's supply path (STOP.md: 216 mΩ, 109 of 123 mV supply side) and its ground. U32's branch carries microamps.
- So, if the tap is upstream, when U32 asserts at 3003 mV, U30's VDD−VSS can be lower by U30's whole drop from the tap point:
  - by the gate's own semantics, up to the rail's 145 mV drop limit: 3003 − 145 = **2858 mV**;
  - on this copper, up to the certified 220.8 mV: about **2782 mV**.
- Either way U30 runs below its 3.0 V (v1.7 p. 21, Table 6-2) while EN is high. That is exactly the case the work order exists to remove.

**The concrete failing input:** `examples/sentinel-03.json` as committed. Readiness passes U30 (`rail_monitor_4413.rs::sentinel_03_validates_and_u32_holds_its_radio_rail`), and the hold is not guaranteed at U30's pin.

**The rule.** The comparison has 3 mV of margin and no term for the drop between the monitor and the part.
- The same drop-free comparison is `4310`'s rule for NRST. There it was harmless, because the margins were hundreds of mV.
- Here it decides the outcome.
- A sound rule would be `VIT− min − (drop from the monitor to the part) ≥ part minimum`. Bounded only by the rail's limit, TPS3839G33 cannot meet it (2858 < 3000).
- That leaves two options for the architect:
  - (a) Bind U32's VDD/GND to U30's pads (an anchor, or a Kelvin tap). Name the residual pad-to-pad drop as a condition, and credit only a co-located monitor.
  - (b) Re-open D1. The fix is a ruling, not a builder tweak, because it touches `4310`'s floor rule.
- The FEEDBACK draft (§4, last row) names only that "its distance is not bounded". It does not name that the credit depends on it.

## B. Answers to §5

### Q1. A monitor that isn't one

**`held`** (`supervisor.rs:73`):
- It credits:
  - a module only if its `enable.monitor` names the monitor;
  - a translator side only if its `OE` control names it and that side's rail is the monitor's;
  - the monitor itself.
- `judged_against`'s closure also requires `m.rail == rail`.
- `check_rail_monitors` refuses, at validate:
  - a name with no monitor;
  - a monitor of another rail;
  - a regulator control naming one;
  - a monitor that drives nothing.
- So nothing is credited that the netlist does not drive (the emitter wires EN and OE to `<ref>_RESET`). **But "drives" is not "holds at that part's pin": see A1.**
- The self-credit of U32 is circular but harmless. It passes at VDD 900 mV, and RESET is asserted down to V(POR) 0.6 V (SBVS193D p. 12, §8.4.2).

**U30 held by EN low:**
- CHIP_EN low means the chip is powered off (v1.7 p. 23, power-off row).
- VIL_nRST ≤ 0.25 × VDD (p. 22, Table 6-3). TPS3839's VOL ≤ 0.4 V at VDD 2.8–6.5 V, IOL 2 mA (p. 7) clears it.
- Below VDD ≈ 1.6 V, 0.4 V can exceed 0.25 × VDD, and below 0.6 V RESET is undefined. The module is far out of range there anyway, so this is a gap only.
- tRST ≥ 50 µs (p. 15) is met by td ≥ 120 ms.
- tSTBL 50 µs is met by `delay_min_microseconds` 120000 (`supervisor.rs:870`, guard and mutant R-M14).

**U31.B held by OE low:**
- OE low places all outputs, A and B, in high-impedance (SCES935A pin table; p. 20, §9.1).
- OE is overvoltage-tolerant with VCC(MIN) circuitry (p. 21, §9.3.2; p. 22, §9.3.6).
- The floor compares VCCB's operating 1080 mV (p. 6, §7.3) against 3003, so that is trivially met. When VCCB < 3003, U32 holds OE low, which also covers the 3.0–3.6 V stated rows.
- **Gap:**
  - 9.3.5's isolation is stated only under 100 mV.
  - OE is now released 120 ms after `VDD_3V3_RF` is good, not tied to VCCA.
  - Nothing judges that VCCA (`VDD_3V3`, U11 after U13's PG) is up before then. U11's start time against the 120 ms is not judged anywhere. `CrossingOeUnheld` (`power.rs:1813-1824`) accepts any VCCB monitor.

**Thresholds:**
- VIT− minimum 3003 (−40 to 85 °C, p. 7, §7.5) is the right worst-case figure against 3000.
- The release, VIT− max 3126 + Vhys typ 31 = 3157, is used identically in `check_nrst` (`:998`) and `check_rail_monitors` (`:830`), with strict `>=` refusal (mutant R-M12 killed).
- Vhys is typical only. Acknowledged, with bench row B-10.

**Transient gaps (not modelled):**
- TPS3839 rejects short dips (p. 12, Fig. 13).
- `tPD_VDD` is a single 20 µs figure (p. 8). Its column (typical or maximum) is not recoverable from the text.
- So the module can run briefly under 3.0 V during a TX-burst droop.

**The rail's minimum at the monitor's pin** (`rail_minimum`, `:656`):
- It is the solved minimum truncated to whole mV (3303), less the limit (145): 3158. That is conservative for the release.
- U32 sits on a microamp branch, so the release check is sound. The hold check is the one that is wrong (A1).
- **Inconsistency:** with a solved domain but no drop limit, it falls back to the declared minimum, while `check_nrst` uses the solved minimum less 0 (`:974-996`).
  - A radio rail cannot reach this branch: `DropLimitUndeclared` fires first (`power.rs` `radio_rails`).
  - A monitor driving only a `power.crossings` OE on an unlimited rail would. It has no test and no mutant.

| Consumer | Maximum | Result |

  - U30's TX load steps (its row's 505 mA) make overshoot beyond 10.9 mV plausible, at an absolute maximum. No transient figure is held.

  - The pack's minimum is 9000 mV, less about 84 mV of input path, so it passes by the existing gate.
  - FB (0.985–1.015 V) and IFB 50 nA × 100 kΩ are in the solve (`power.rs:1386-1404`).

### Q4. A drop limit re-derived toward the answer

- **No.** 145 is "the solved minimum less U32's release, strictly under", the same rule as VDD_3V3's 110 (`4300k` D6, its source text at `sentinel-03.json`).
- By the old rule (the solved minimum less what must hold, U30's 3000), it would be 303 mV, and the 220.8 mV bound would pass. The build took the stricter rule, and the board stays refused.
- **Strictness:** 3303 − 146 = 3157, which is not under 3157, so 146 is refused. 145 is the edge (planted, R-M12).
- 146 fails only because the solved 3303.185 is truncated to 3303. On exact µV, 146 would pass by 0.185 mV. The truncation is conservative, and FEEDBACK §3 names the 145-versus-ruled-146 deviation.

## C. Other checks

**Refusal ordering:**
- A translator OE naming an undeclared monitor (a typo) is refused as `CrossingOeUnheld` (power::check, step 16), before `RailMonitorInvalid` (step 16a) could name the typo.

- "Rail above a consumer's maximum" is caught by U31's row (`DomainOutsideConsumer`) before U30's own `RadioSupplyOutOfRange` (step 27).
- All of these fail safe. None masks a pass.

**validate versus readiness:**
- The hold (VIT− minimum against the part's minimum) is judged only in readiness (`judged`). `validate`, `generate` and `emit` accept a rail monitor that holds nothing at its part's minimum.
- Path B refuses its bus devices at validate (`SupervisorBelowDeviceFloor`). The README's v32 paragraph lists no threshold refusal, so it is consistent, but it is a difference to name.

**Guards with no test or mutant:**
- A monitor part that is unknown or not a supervisor row (`PowerPartUnknown` at `check_rail_monitors`). Only the bypass and open-drain branches are planted.
- The non-wirable `monitor` arm, `power.rs:4830`, and its emitter twin in `schematic_power.rs` `controls()`.
- `SupervisorOutOfRange`'s `rail_min < vdd_lo` half.
- `rail_minimum`'s fallback, which is exercised only by id in `readiness_4300k`.
- "is no voltage domain" (`:828`), unreachable after `shape`'s `known()`.
- No mutant for `judged_against`'s `m.rail == rail` or the `p.rail != mcu.supply` guard. Both are redundant after validate.

**Nets:**

**The TPS3839 row and symbol:**

**The CI job** (`ci.yml:627-688`):
