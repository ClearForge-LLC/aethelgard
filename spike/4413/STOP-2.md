## A1: U32's hold on U30 is not true at U30's pin (blocking)

- **My D1 said U32 "is placed at U30 and senses the rail at U30's pin". Nothing I built does that.** The emitter anchors only C95 to U32. On the regenerated board U32 is at (75.4, 100.15), 83 mm from U30 at (129.04, 36.8) and 11 mm from U31.
- **The floor rule (`supervisor::judged_against`) passes U30 because U32's VIT− minimum of 3003 mV is at or above U30's 3000.** That is a margin of 3 mV, with no term for the drop between U32's pins and U30's.
- **U30 draws its 505 mA through the rail's supply path and ground; U32 draws microamps.** If U32 taps the rail upstream of U30, U30 can sit below U32 by its own drop while EN is high:
  - by the gate's own limit, 3003 − 145 = 2858 mV;
  - on this copper, about 2782 mV (the certified 220.8 mV bound).

  Either way U30 runs under its 3.0 V with EN high, which is the case the work order exists to remove. Where U32's branch joins the rail is not traced.
- **The sound rule is VIT− minimum − (drop from the monitor to the part) ≥ the part's minimum.** It touches `4310`'s floor rule, outside this fence. The same drop-free comparison serves NRST, where the margins are hundreds of mV.
- **Options:**
  - **(a) Bind U32 to U30.** U32 is owned by U30, as U13 is, so it travels with the module's group. Its VDD and GND are tapped at U30's 3V3 and ground pads by their own short tracks, a Kelvin tap. The floor rule credits a monitor only when it is tapped at the part's pads, and the residual (the tap's microamps through the tap track) is named as a condition, with a bench row reading EN against VDD at U30's pads.
    - The cost is placer and router work: a second owned IC in the radio group is untested, and the survey shows that anchoring an IC to U30 breaks the grouping.
    - It also changes `4310`'s rule.
  - **(b) Judge the hold at the part's pin from the copper:** a certified pad-to-pad bound from the monitor's VDD to the part's supply under the part's current, in `extract`'s supply gate. This is engine work, and with TPS3839G33's 3 mV it passes only if that bound is under 3 mV, which an upper bound on this copper will not give unless the tap is beside the pad.
  - **(c) Re-open D1.** No held part fits once a drop term is honest, because any monitor away from U30 needs VIT− minimum ≥ 3000 + 145, with a release under 3.6 V less the band. That is impossible at an LMR33620's 286 mV band.
- **My lean: (a), folded into the ground-split engine work order (D6).** Both are placement and `4310`-gate work on the same board, and the readiness credit is false until it lands.
  - Until then, Sentinel-03 should be refused by readiness again, by name: a monitor not tapped at its part is no monitor. Built today, the floor rule credits it, and that should not merge as true.
  - So this branch either (i) reverts the credit, keeping the part, the rail and the field, so readiness names U30 again, or (ii) waits for (a). **Your call.**
